The players will start from [[Willowbrook]], they all live around the area and are drawn together for the renowed [[Willowbrook#Annual Harvest Festival]] to seek entertainment. 


# Plot hooks

Once the players are in Willowbrook, they will join the festivities until prompted by one of the [[Willowbrook#Current Issues and Plot Hooks]] from Willowbrook. The players can choose to accept these plot hooks and investigate. 


# Pre-game knowledge
The party is in a very remote location, and know of two Villages nearby
