## Introduction
Welcome to your new adventure! In the world of Sunset! You find yourselves in the quaint village of Willowbrook, a place known for its lush landscapes, friendly locals, and the Annual Harvest Festival. Here, you can start anew, explore the world, and uncover the mysteries that lie within and around the village.

## Background
You were captives on a pirate ship, each taken from different locations and backgrounds. During a fierce storm, the ship ran aground near Willowbrook. In the chaos, you managed to escape and swim to shore. Now free but unsure of your next steps, you find yourselves in Willowbrook during its most festive time.

**The Harvest Festival:**
- **Activities:** Gambling, Games, Dances, Contests, Markets, Performances, all with handsome rewards (and XP)!
- **Atmosphere:** A joyous occasion with music, dancing, feasting, and various competitions.
- **Opportunities:** Blend in with the crowd, enjoy the festivities, and gather information.

### Starting Inventory

- **Basic Equipment:** Each player starts with basic clothing, a small amount of gold (10 gp), and one personal item of significance. 

- **Pirates' Booty**: Amidst the chaos of the storm, you each managed to plunder one item from the Pirate's ship. Can be *anything*, as long as it's realistic.

- **Survival Gear:** A basic set of tools for survival, such as a knife, flint, and rope.

- **Starting level**: 1 :) (Is open voor discussie)

## Starting Location: Willowbrook

### Overview:
- **Population:** 150-200 residents
- **Known For:** Fertile lands, Annual Harvest Festival
- **Atmosphere:** Quaint and picturesque with well-maintained cottages, blooming gardens, and a strong sense of community.
### Key Locations:
- **Village Square:** The heart of Willowbrook where the Harvest Festival is held.
- **[[The Resting Oak Inn]]:** Run by [[Pippa Quickfoot]], this inn provides a warm and inviting space for travelers.
- **Smithy:** Operated by [[Thorn Battlehammer]], offering weapons, armor, and repairs.
- **Herbalist’s Hut:** [[Marigold Thistlefoot]]’s home, surrounded by a vibrant garden of herbs and flowers.
- **Elder’s Hall:** The administrative center where [[Elder Bramble]] holds council.

## Immediate Goals
1. **Blend In:** Participate in the Harvest Festival to gather information and establish a presence in Willowbrook.
2. **Gather Resources:** Find food, shelter, and equipment to prepare for future adventures.
3. **Seek Allies:** Connect with local NPCs who might provide aid, information, or quests.
4. **Explore:** Investigate the local area and get set up!
