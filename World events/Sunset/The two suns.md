
## The time of twin suns (2000 years ago)
Originally, the world of sunset had two suns, [[Helionis]] and [[Lunaris]]. 

[[Helionis]] was the larger and brighter of the two, governing the day whilst providing vibrant energy for growth and activity.

[[Lunaris]] was the smaller and gentler sun, governing the night. It provided a calm influence, fostering rest and reflection.

## The corruption (1500 years ago)
During it's cosmic journey, the world of Sunset encountered [[Umbraxis]]. A dark deity that corrupted the bigger of the two suns, Helionis. This left Helionis corrupted and forever changed. It's light, once pure and life-giving, became a source of blight. The corruption of Helionis had profound effects on its twin [[Lunaris]].

## The fall of Lunaris (1200 years ago)
As [[Helionis]]'s corruption spread, it began to consume its twin, Lunaris. The delicate balance that had existed between the two suns was shattered. The harmonious light of Lunaris was gradually overpowered by the blighted light of Helionis. Creating [[Blight Gems]] and [[Blight Entity]]es wherever the rays managed to go.

During this event, Lunaris attempted to resist the corruption, in what was known as [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]]. In a final, desperate act, [[Lunaris]] channeled all its remaning energy to push Helionis away, creating a momentary rift in the cosmos. After this event, Helionis became the sole sun, its corrupted light now dominating the skies of Sunset. 

## The Age of Blight (1150 years ago)
This event was closely monitored by a group of mages in the [[The Sunken Citadel|The Citadel of Luminae]]. Where [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]] was dubbed [[Lunaris’s Last Stand|The Sundering]]. The Mages in [[The Sunken Citadel|The Citadel of Luminae]] managed to harvest some of the power that was exerted by [[Lunaris]]. A group of researchers, led by [[Archmage P]] managed convert the harvested power of [[Lunaris]] into a core, that was able to nullify some of [[Helionis]]'s influence. This core was at the center of the [[The Sunken Citadel|The Citadel of Luminae]], where it stayed for centuries. Beyond nullifying the rays of [[Helionis]], it even managed to weaken the influence of [[Umbraxis]] on it. Although further research was never achieved. 

## The fall of Luminae (1120 years ago)
Throughout the years, the power of [[The Solar Core]] became known, and drew a group of suspicious actors, who tried to harvest power from the core. After creating a set of legendary weaponry and stashing it away in something only known as "[[The Golden Vault]]", the core became unstable. This unleashed a chain reaction of catastrophes, resulting in the city of Luminae sinking into the depts. 

This event marked the end of the ancient civilization, and the beginning of Luminae's descent into legend.

### The Present Day
Over the centuries, the world of Sunset has forgotten the true purpose of the Solar Core and the events that led to Luminae's fall. The blight, although diminished by the residual power of the Solar Core, never fully disappeared and has recently begun to spread once more. The weakening power of the Solar Core has allowed Helionis’s influence to grow stronger, accelerating the corruption across the world.

Helionis's blighted rays have corrupted the minds of many, voiding memories all have of the time when [[Helionis]] was still pure. 


If enough blight exists on the world, [[Umbraxis]]'s corruption will fully take over [[Helionis]] and show its true self. As the permanent night's sky. No moon, but instead, an enormous prying eye hovering in the sky. Observing, watching, corrupting.
