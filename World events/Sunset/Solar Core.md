### Overview
The **Solar Core** was a powerful artifact created by the ancient mages of the [[The Sunken Citadel|Citadel of Luminae]] to counteract the corrupting influence of [[Helionis]] and maintain balance in the world of [[Sunset]]. It was a beacon of pure energy, designed to filter and purify the rays of Helionis, ensuring that its light remained life-giving and free from the taint of [[Umbraxis]].

### Creation by Archmage P

The Solar Core was the brainchild of the renowned and enigmatic **Archmage P**, who led a dedicated group of researchers in the Citadel of Luminae. The creation process involved harnessing the last vestiges of [[Lunaris]]'s energy during [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]] and combining it with powerful enchantments and runes to form a stabilizing core. The Solar Core was a marvel of magical engineering and ancient wisdom, representing the pinnacle of the citadel's arcane knowledge.

### Function

- **Purification:** The Solar Core filtered the blighted rays of Helionis, purifying them and preventing the spread of corruption.
- **Stabilization:** It maintained the balance between the light and darkness, ensuring that Helionis's influence did not overpower the world of Sunset.
- **Protection:** The core created a protective aura around the Citadel of Luminae, shielding it from external threats and the blight.

### Legendary Equipment Created by the Solar Core

Before the catastrophic event that sank the city of Luminae, the Solar Core was used to create a set of legendary equipment. These artifacts were designed to harness the core's pure energy and provide unmatched power and protection to their wielders.

#### Types of Legendary Equipment
##### Spell Scrolls
- **Scroll of Solar Flare:** Unleashes a powerful burst of pure light that blinds and damages enemies.
- **Scroll of Celestial Shield:** Creates an impenetrable barrier of light that protects against all forms of attack.
#####  Weapons
- **Sunsword:** A radiant blade that cuts through darkness and blight, dealing extra radiant damage to corrupted creatures.
- **Lunar Bow:** A bow that fires arrows of pure light, able to pierce through the toughest defenses and dispel illusions.
##### Armor
- **Armor of the Dawn:** A set of gleaming plate armor that grants resistance to necrotic damage and provides a radiant aura that heals allies.
- **Moonlit Cloak:** A cloak that allows the wearer to become invisible in twilight and provides protection against mind-altering effects.
##### Spell Tomes
- **Tome of Solar Magic:** Contains advanced spells that manipulate light and energy, enhancing the caster's abilities to control the battlefield.
- **Tome of Lunar Secrets:** A collection of hidden knowledge and spells that protect against dark magic and enhance divination abilities.
##### Attire
- **Robes of the Luminary:** Elegant robes that amplify the wearer's magical abilities and provide a constant source of light.
- **Boots of the Celestial Wanderer:** Boots that grant the wearer incredible speed and the ability to walk on air.
### The Fall of Luminae
Despite its incredible power, the Solar Core eventually became unstable due to misuse and external interference. Suspicious actors, drawn by its power, attempted to harness it for their own purposes. This led to the creation of the legendary equipment stored in the [[The Golden Vault]], but also triggered a catastrophic chain reaction.

- **Chain Reaction:** The unstable core unleashed a series of magical catastrophes that tore through the citadel. Instead of blocking out the [[The Blight]] rays, it temporarily enhanced the blight, spreading blight all through the city. 
- **Sinking of Luminae:** The city of Luminae was engulfed by these events, causing it to sink into the depths of the earth and become the Sunken Citadel.

### Legacy

The Solar Core's legacy lives on through the legendary equipment it created and the myths surrounding its power. The remnants of its protective aura still linger in the Sunken Citadel, offering a glimmer of hope to those who seek to uncover its secrets and restore balance to the world of Sunset.
