## Origins of Helionis

**Helionis** is a cosmic deity of immense power, originally a benevolent entity that brought warmth and life to the world of Sunset. Helionis traveled across the cosmos, bestowing light and energy upon the worlds it encountered, fostering growth and prosperity.

However, during its journey, Helionis encountered a dark celestial force known as **[[Umbraxis]]**, a corrupting entity that sought to spread chaos and decay. In their encounter, Helionis was tainted by [[Umbraxis]]'s corruption. This event twisted Helionis’s once-pure light into a source of blight, transforming it from a bringer of life into a harbinger of decay.
