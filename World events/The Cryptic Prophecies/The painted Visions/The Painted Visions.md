### Characteristics of the Painted Visions:

1. **Style and Appearance:**
    - **Abstract and Surreal:** The paintings often have an abstract, surreal quality, with distorted landscapes, unnatural colors, and figures that blend into their surroundings in strange ways.
    - **Unsettling Imagery:** Common elements include twisted trees, sunken cities, shadowy figures, eyes in the sky, and glowing crystals. The colors are often muted with bursts of bright, unnatural hues, especially sickly greens and purples.
    - **Hidden Details:** Each painting contains subtle, hidden details that might not be immediately obvious—faces hidden in the background, symbols woven into the brushstrokes, or scenes within scenes that become clear only upon closer inspection.
2. **Language and Symbols:**
    - **Ancient Runes:** Some paintings include inscriptions in ancient languages or runes that can be deciphered by those with knowledge of history or magic.
    - **Blighted Symbols:** Symbols tied to the blight and **Umbraxis** are often present, such as the eye, twisted roots, and fractured suns.
    - **Prophetic Phrases:** Scrawled phrases in various languages may be found near or within the paintings, often cryptic, fragmented, and open to interpretation.
3. **Effects on Viewers:**
    - **Psychic Whispers:** Those who gaze upon the paintings for too long might hear faint whispers or experience fleeting visions related to the blight or the future.
    - **Compelling Urges:** Some viewers might feel compelled to seek out certain locations or perform specific actions hinted at by the painting.
    - **Minor Madness:** Prolonged exposure to the paintings could lead to temporary madness, causing paranoia or hallucinations, reflecting the experiences of the original artists.
