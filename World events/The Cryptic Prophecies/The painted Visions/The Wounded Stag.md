- **Location:** Painted on the wall of a hunter's lodge, hidden behind a tapestry.
- **Description:** A noble stag stands wounded in a forest clearing, its blood forming a trail that leads into the dark woods. The forest seems alive with eyes watching from the shadows.
- **Prophecy:** "The stag bleeds, the forest watches. Where the trail ends, the hunt begins."
- **Clue:** This painting could be a metaphor for being pursued by an unseen force or a literal hunt for a creature connected to the blight.
