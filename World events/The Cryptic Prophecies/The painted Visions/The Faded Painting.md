#### **Quest Start: The Faded Painting**
- **Trigger:** The quest begins when the players interact with Borin Ironhand, the blacksmith. He mentions an old, faded painting he found in his attic, passed down through his family. He donated it to the inn, but thinks there's more to it.
- **Borin’s Dialogue:** "There's this one thing...."

#### Investigating the Painting
- **Painting Description:** The painting shows a serene scene of Willowbrook from long ago, focusing on the village square. However, some details don’t match the current village, and the colors seem to shift slightly when viewed from different angles. The painting seems to whisper faintly, but the words are unintelligible.
- **Insight:** A successful Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Perception) check (DC 12) reveals faint hints hidden within the brushstrokes, visible only under the right light.

#### Step 1: The First Hint
- **Hint on the Painting:** "Where water rests in stone, seek the mark left behind."
    - **Location:** The village well.
- **Interaction at the Well:** Upon investigating the well in the village square, the players find a small, hidden inscription on the stone rim. The inscription reads: "Where the old watches the young, something waits beneath."

#### Step 2: The Whispering Willow
- **Hint at the Whispering Willow:** "Under the shade of the ancient oak, something lies buried deep.
    - **Location:** The ancient oak tree near the Resting Oak Inn.
- **Interaction:** The players must locate the ancient oak tree near the Resting Oak Inn. Beneath the oak tree, they find a patch of earth that looks off. Digging here reveals a buried stone box.

#### Step 3: The Stone Box
- **Box Description:** The stone box is sealed and covered in intricate carvings. There is no obvious way to open it, but there is another hint inscribed on the lid: "By the fire's warmth, the truth is revealed.
    - **Location:** The Resting Oak Inn.
- **Interaction:** If the players bring the box to the Resting Oak Inn and place it near a lit lantern, the carvings on the box shift, and the lid opens, revealing an old key and a small, folded map. The map is a rough sketch of Willowbrook, with a single location marked — the Elder's Hall.

#### Step 4: The Hidden Chamber
- **The Elder’s Hall:** The players follow the map to the Elder’s Hall. The map indicates a spot behind the building, near an old storage room. The players must search this area to find a hidden door, accessible only with the key they found
- **The Hidden Door:** Behind the storage room, concealed by vines and old debris, is a small door. The key fits perfectly into the lock, and opening the door reveals a narrow staircase leading down to a hidden chamber.
- **The Hidden Chamber:** The chamber is small and dimly lit, with walls covered in old tapestries depicting the history of Willowbrook. In the center of the room is a pedestal with a small chest, unlocked.
- **Unlocking the Mystery:**
    - **Inside the Chest:** The chest contains a finely crafted pendant, an old journal, and a small pouch of gold.
    - **The Pendant:** The pendant grants +1 to Wisdom saving throws and has an engraving of an ancient tree, likely the Whispering Willow.
    - **The Journal:** [[The journal of Borin's Ancestor]] contains writings from an ancestor of Borin Ironhand, describing how the pendant was hidden to protect it from a dark force that once threatened Willowbrook. It also hints at deeper secrets connected to the Whispering Willow and the village’s ancient past.

#### Final Reward and Wrap-Up

- **Returning to Borin:** If the players return to Borin with the pendant or tell him of their discovery, he thanks them and offers to upgrade one of their weapons or armor as a reward for their efforts. The journal can also be used to uncover more secrets or quests within Willowbrook.
- **Potential Plot Hook:** The journal hints at a forgotten history of Willowbrook and the protection offered by the Whispering Willow, potentially leading to future quests related to the ancient tree and the blight.

#### **XP and Rewards**
- **Solving the Hints:** 25 XP per player for each hint solved.
- **Discovering the Hidden Chamber:** 50 XP per player.
- **Unlocking the Chest and Recovering the Treasure:** 100 XP per player.
- **Additional Rewards:**
    - **Pendant:** Grants +1 to Wisdom saving throws.
    - **20 Gold Pieces**
    - **The Journal:** A potential source of future quests or lore.
