**Location:** Hidden in the ice of a frozen mountain cave.
- **Description:** A single flame burns within a block of ice, casting an eerie blue light. The ice is cracked, with dark veins spreading outward.
- **Prophecy:** "The flame burns cold, the ice cracks. When fire and ice meet, the world shall tremble."
- **Clue:** This might symbolize a clash of opposing forces or the release of a powerful entity trapped for centuries.
