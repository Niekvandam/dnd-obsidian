- **Location:** Discovered in the ruins of an old blacksmith’s forge.
- **Description:** A chain, once strong and unbreakable, is shown shattering into pieces. Each link falls away into darkness, where shadowy hands reach out to grasp them.
- **Prophecy:** "The chain breaks, the bonds are loosed. What was held shall be free."
- **Clue:** This could symbolize the breaking of an ancient pact or the release of something bound, perhaps linked to the [[The Sunken Citadel]] or a **[[Blight Entity]]**.
