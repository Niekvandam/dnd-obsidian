- **Location:** Hidden in an overgrown garden within an old manor.
- **Description:** A sunflower, towering above all others, has a face twisted in agony where its center should be. The petals are darkening, and the surrounding flowers are wilting.
- **Prophecy:** "The flower screams as the sun fades. Darkness spreads where light once shone."
- **Clue:** This might be a warning about the spreading corruption of [[the blight]], particularly in areas tied to [[Helionis]].
