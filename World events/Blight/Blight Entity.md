## Blight entities
Blight entities will spawn from Blight Gems, and will arise in certain locations. They all obey Umbraxis, the deity that corrupted Helionis. But, they are still influenced by Patrons. The Blight entity will have a severe effect on the region it is in. It might mutate animals, poison the lands. Wither the plants. cloudy, dusty, rainy areas, absurd weather effects etc. These will come in three stages. Stage 1, 2 and 3, in each the level of blight will be even more present.

## List of blight entities

### [[Infernal Devourer]]

**Patron:** [[The Fiend]] **Language:** Abyssal

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** The land becomes scorched and dry, with small fires spontaneously igniting. Animals become aggressive and are more prone to violence.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The air crackles with heat as the ground begins to split. Ash drifts like dark snowflakes, and the once-green fields smolder under an oppressive heat."

- **Stage 2:** The air becomes thick with ash and smoke. Plants wither and die, and the ground cracks. Infernal creatures start appearing, causing chaos.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Thick smoke fills the lungs as flames dance across the landscape. The screams of infernal creatures echo through the choking haze, a symphony of chaos and destruction."

- **Stage 3:** The sky darkens with perpetual smoke clouds. Rivers and lakes boil, and the temperature rises to unbearable levels. Entire areas become lava-filled wastelands.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sun is blotted out by a sky of roiling smoke. Rivers of molten lava carve new paths through the land, and the very air burns with a hellish intensity."

**Abilities of Infernal Devourer:**

- **Fire Aura:** Emits an aura of intense heat that deals fire damage to those nearby.
- **Infernal Roar:** A terrifying roar that causes fear in all creatures within a certain radius.
- **Flame Strike:** Summons a pillar of fire to engulf its enemies.
### [[Eldritch Aberration]]

**Patron:** [[The Great Old One]] **Language:** Deep Speech

**Effect on Region:**
- **Stage 1:** The weather becomes erratic, with sudden storms and unnatural calm periods. Animals exhibit strange behaviors and mutations.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sky churns with ominous clouds, and the air hums with an unnatural energy. Creatures twist and writhe, their forms defying nature."

- **Stage 2:** The landscape starts to twist and warp, with geometry defying natural laws. Plants grow in bizarre, unnatural shapes, and hallucinations become common.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Reality itself seems to bend and shift. Trees grow sideways, and the ground seems to pulse with a life of its own. Strange visions haunt the mind."

- **Stage 3:** Reality itself seems to break down, with portals to other dimensions opening randomly. The sky shifts colors unnaturally, and the ground pulsates as if alive.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Portals to otherworldly realms tear through the fabric of reality. The landscape is a chaotic swirl of colors and shapes, defying all logic and reason."

**Abilities of Eldritch Aberration:**

- **Mind Warp:** Causes confusion and hallucinations in its enemies.
- **Dimensional Rift:** Opens portals that can transport creatures to other dimensions.
- **Warp Reality:** Alters the terrain in unpredictable and dangerous ways.
### [[Necrotic Wraith]]

**Patron:** [[The Undead]] **Language:** Infernal

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** A chll permeates the area, and fog rises from the ground. Plants and animals start to die off, and the dead rise as zombies or skeletons.
    - **Flavour Text:** "A bone-chilling cold seeps into the bones, and a dense fog blankets the land. The stench of decay is pervasive, and the dead stir in their graves."

- **Stage 2:** The fog thickens, reducing visibility. The air is filled with the stench of decay. Ghosts and wraiths begin to haunt the region, draining life from the living.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The fog grows so thick it feels like a living entity. Ghostly apparitions drift through the mists, their touch draining the warmth of life from all they encounter."

- **Stage 3:** The area becomes a necropolis, with undead armies patrolling the land. All living things are either dead or undead, and the sky is perpetually overcast.
    - **Flavour Text:** "An endless army of the undead roams the land, turning it into a vast necropolis. The sky is a constant gray, and the living find no refuge here."

**Abilities of Necrotic Wraith:**

- **Life Drain:** Steals life force from living creatures to heal itself.
- **Necrotic Shroud:** Surrounds itself in an aura of necrotic energy that weakens those nearby.
- **Raise Dead:** Can raise fallen creatures as undead minions.
### [[Abyssal Leviathan]]

**Patron:** [[The Fathomless]] **Language:** Aquan

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** Heavy rains and floods begin to plague the area. Water sources become tainted and salty, and aquatic creatures grow larger and more aggressive.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Endless rain turns the land to mire, and streams swell into raging torrents. Fish and other aquatic creatures grow monstrous and hostile."

- **Stage 2:** Permanent flooding submerges large portions of the land. Aquatic monsters appear frequently, and the air is perpetually humid and thick.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The land is swallowed by water, transforming fields into swamps. The roar of monstrous beasts echoes through the perpetual mist."

- **Stage 3:** The area is completely underwater, with air pockets being rare. Underwater predators dominate, and the landscape is a drowned wasteland.
    - **Flavour Text:** "An underwater realm of darkness and predation. The drowned lands are ruled by vicious sea creatures, and pockets of air are precious and rare."

**Abilities of Abyssal Leviathan:**

- **Water Manipulation:** Controls water to create floods and whirlpools.
- **Abyssal Roar:** A roar that creates shockwaves underwater, damaging structures and creatures.
- **Tentacle Slam:** Uses powerful tentacles to crush enemies and structures.

### [[Fallen Guardian]]

**Patron:** [[The Celestial (Corrupted)]] **Language:** Celestial (twisted)

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** Plants start growing uncontrollably, covering everything in thick vines and thorny bushes. The sky is perpetually cloudy, with occasional blinding flashes of light.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Vegetation runs rampant, turning paths into tangled thickets. The sky is an ever-shifting canvas of clouds and blinding light."

- **Stage 2:** The vegetation becomes sentient and hostile, attacking any who enter. The ground is covered in a thick layer of vines and thorns, making travel difficult.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The plants awaken, their vines and thorns acting with malevolent intent. Every step is a battle against the choking greenery."

- **Stage 3:** The area is overgrown to the point of being impenetrable. Giant, hostile plant creatures roam the land, and the sky is filled with a permanent, blinding light.
    - **Flavour Text:** "An impenetrable jungle of living nightmares. Giant plant creatures patrol the overgrown ruins, and the light is blinding and relentless."

**Abilities of Fallen Guardian:**

- **Vine Whip:** Uses thorny vines to attack and restrain enemies.
- **Photosynthesis:** Heals itself in the presence of light.
- **Rooted Call:** Summons hostile plant creatures to aid in battle.

### [[Dark Muse]]
**Patron:** [[The Hexblade]] **Language:** Infernal and whispers

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** Shadows grow longer and darker, with an oppressive feeling in the air. Small, shadowy creatures begin to appear and cause minor disturbances.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Shadows seem to reach out, and the air grows heavy with unseen dread. Tiny, impish figures flit just at the edge of sight."

- **Stage 2:** The darkness deepens, with visibility reduced even during the day. Shadowy figures stalk the land, and people feel a constant sense of dread and paranoia.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Daylight is swallowed by darkness, and shadowy figures haunt every corner. A pervasive sense of dread grips the heart."

- **Stage 3:** The land is covered in perpetual twilight, with powerful shadow creatures dominating the area. The landscape itself seems to shift and change, making navigation difficult.
    - **Flavour Text:** "A realm of eternal twilight, where shadowy beasts rule and the very ground seems to conspire against you. Every step feels uncertain and treacherous."

**Abilities of Dark Muse:**

- **Shadow Step:** Teleports between shadows, making it hard to track or hit.
- **Whispering Fear:** Sends whispers that induce fear and paranoia.
- **Dark Embrace:** Envelops an area in darkness, blinding and disorienting those within.
### [[Corrupted Elemental]]

**Patron:** [[The Genie (Efreet)]] **Language:** Primordial

**Effect on Region:**
- **Stage 1:** The air becomes hot and dry, with small dust storms occurring frequently. Fires start more easily, and water sources begin to dry up.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The heat is unrelenting, and the air carries the sting of sand. Water becomes scarce as the land dries and cracks."

- **Stage 2:** Large portions of the land become desert-like, with constant sandstorms. Plants and animals either adapt to the harsh conditions or die.
    - **Flavour Text:** "Sandstorms rage across an arid wasteland. The flora and fauna struggle to survive in the relentless heat and choking dust."


- **Stage 3:** The area becomes a vast desert, with scorching temperatures and massive dunes. Fire elementals and other fiery creatures roam the land, and oases are rare.
    - **Flavour Text:** "A desert of fire and sand, where only the fiercest creatures survive. The land is a sea of dunes under a burning sky."

**Abilities of Corrupted Elemental:**

- **Fire Blast:** Launches powerful fire attacks that incinerate enemies.
- **Sandstorm:** Creates blinding sandstorms that disorient and damage.
- **Heatwave:** Generates intense heat that weakens and exhausts foes.

### [[Twilight Phantom]]
**Patron:** [[The Celestial (Corrupted)]] **Language:** Celestial and haunting echoes

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:** The region experiences extended twilight, with the sun never fully setting. People and animals become more active at night, and the air feels lighter.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sun hangs low, casting the world in a perpetual twilight. Night blooms with activity, and the air is filled with a strange vibrancy."

- **Stage 2:** The twilight becomes perpetual, with a constant dim light. Plants begin to glow faintly, and nocturnal creatures thrive.
    - **Flavour Text:** "The world is awash in dim light, where plants glow softly and night creatures reign supreme. A mystical, otherworldly glow suffuses everything."

- **Stage 3:** The land is bathed in eternal twilight, with magical bioluminescent plants and creatures lighting the way. The environment is calm and peaceful, but outsiders may find it disorienting.
    - **Flavour Text:** "An eternal twilight, where the land is illuminated by bioluminescent flora and fauna. The air is calm and serene, but the unending twilight can be disorienting."

**Abilities of Twilight Phantom:**

- **Ethereal Glide:** Moves silently and can pass through solid objects.
- **Twilight Aura:** Creates an area of dim light that confuses and weakens enemies.
- **Luminous Call:** Summons bioluminescent creatures to fight alongside it.



### [[The Brittle one]]

**Patron:** [[The Celestial (Corrupted)]] **Language:** Celestial and haunting echoes
- **Stage 1**: In this initial stage, the cavern walls and rock formations are subtly weakened and cracked, with chunks occasionally breaking off. The goblins, their bodies thin and somewhat translucent, look as though they might crumble under pressure.
- **Stage 2**: The walls develop spiderweb fractures that occasionally release dust and small stones, while strange echoes reverberate as if the cave itself has a hollow, spectral quality. Larger portions of the cave ceiling may begin to collapse, forcing the players and goblins to dodge falling debris. Blighted goblins exhibit slow, erratic movements, like they’re fighting to hold themselves together.
- **Stage 3**: At this level, the entire cavern is a deathtrap, with frequent, dangerous cave-ins. Any remaining goblins are thoroughly mutated, their skin and bones unnaturally brittle, their eyes glowing faintly with an unearthly light. The players must carefully maneuver to avoid crumbling pathways and collapsing tunnels.
