#### Overview

Heartstones are rare and powerful gems that have been deeply influenced by the magical energies of the world of Sunset. Initially, these stones were pure and held great potential for magical enhancement and protection. However, with the corruption of [[Helionis]] and the spread of the blight, many Heartstones have become tainted, transforming into Blighted Heartstones.

#### Origin

Heartstones are believed to have originated from the crystallized essence of [[Lunaris]], the smaller and gentler sun that once governed the night. These crystals were once pure conduits of lunar energy, used by ancient civilizations and powerful mages to create potent magical artifacts.

#### Properties

- **Pure Heartstones:**
    - **Appearance:** Pure Heartstones are typically clear or softly glowing with a silvery or pale blue light. They have a tranquil aura and are often used in rituals and enchantments that require a pure source of magical energy.
    - **Magical Properties:** When used in their pure form, Heartstones can enhance spellcasting abilities, stabilize powerful magical rituals, and provide protection against dark influences. They are especially potent in rituals involving light, protection, and healing.
- **Blighted Heartstones:**
    - **Appearance:** Once corrupted by the blight, Heartstones take on a darker, more sinister appearance. They glow with a sickly green or dark purple light, and their aura feels oppressive and malevolent.
    - **Corrupted Properties:** Blighted Heartstones are tainted with necrotic energy and can amplify dark magic. They are unstable and can cause corruption in living beings and the environment around them. However, if properly purified, they can still be harnessed for their immense power.

#### Lore

The transition from pure to blighted Heartstones is closely tied to the history of [[Helionis]] and [[Lunaris]]. As [[Helionis]] became corrupted by [[Umbraxis]], the balance of power between the two celestial bodies was shattered. This event, known as [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]], marked the beginning of the blight's influence on the world of Sunset.

**Legend of the Heartstone Guardians:** Ancient texts speak of a group of powerful druids and mages known as the Heartstone Guardians. These individuals were entrusted with the task of protecting and preserving the purity of Heartstones. They created sacred groves and hidden sanctuaries where Heartstones were kept safe from corruption. However, with the fall of [[Lunaris]] and the rise of the blight, many of these sanctuaries were lost or overrun by dark forces.

**The Purification Ritual:** Purifying a Blighted Heartstone is a complex and dangerous process. It requires a combination of rare ingredients, powerful magic, and a deep connection to the natural and celestial energies of the world. The purification ritual typically involves:

- **Moonlight Petals:** Flowers that bloom under the light of the full moon, symbolizing the essence of [[Lunaris]].
- **Sunroot:** A mystical plant said to contain the pure rays of [[Helionis]] before its corruption.
- **Sacred Incantations:** Ancient spells and chants passed down through generations of mages and druids.
- **A Pure Heartstone:** Used as a focus to draw out and contain the corruption.

#### Uses

- **Magical Foci:** Both pure and purified Heartstones are used as powerful foci for spellcasters, enhancing their magical abilities and providing stability in complex rituals.
- **Enchanted Artifacts:** Heartstones are often embedded in weapons, armor, and other artifacts to grant magical properties. Pure Heartstones provide protection and healing, while purified Blighted Heartstones offer a balance of power and resistance to corruption.
- **Guardianship:** Some Heartstones are kept in sacred places as guardians against dark forces, their pure energy warding off evil and protecting the land.
