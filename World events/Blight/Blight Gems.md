

## Blight effects

| Dice | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Description                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Paranoid Whispers**: The character hears faint, unsettling whispers that cause paranoia. They have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and saving throws against fear effects for 1d6 days.                                     | Upon touching the gem, the character's eyes widen in fear as they hear soft, eerie whispers echoing in their mind. They frequently glance around nervously, unable to shake the feeling of being watched. |
| 2    | **Pale Skin:** The character's skin becomes unnaturally pale and clammy. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks but advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks for 1d6 weeks.                                           | As the character grips the gem, their skin drains of color, becoming ghostly pale and cool to the touch. Others around them notice their sickly appearance.                                               |
| 3    | **Irritated Rash:** A painful, itchy rash spreads across the character's body. They suffer a -1 penalty to AC due to discomfort for 1d4 days.                                                                                            | Red, inflamed patches appear on the character's skin, causing them to scratch incessantly. The irritation makes it difficult for them to wear armor comfortably.                                          |
| 4    | **Hallucinations:** The character experiences vivid, fleeting hallucinations. They have disadvantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks and must make a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw to distinguish reality from illusion for 1d4 days. | After touching the gem, the character starts seeing fleeting shadows and ghostly figures at the edge of their vision, causing them to second-guess reality.                                               |
| 5    | **Blighted Aura:** A faint aura of darkness surrounds the character. They have advantage on Stealth checks in dim light or darkness for 1d6 days.                                                                                        | Dark tendrils of shadow briefly swirl around the character before settling into a barely perceptible aura, making them blend into the shadows more easily.                                                |
| 6    | **Coughing Fits:** The character develops a persistent cough, making it difficult to speak clearly. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Performance) checks for 1d6 weeks.                                                               | The character starts coughing uncontrollably, their voice becoming hoarse and raspy, making it hard for them to speak or sing.                                                                            |
| 7    | **Nightmares:** The character suffers from intense nightmares, causing exhaustion. They must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw each morning or gain one level of exhaustion. This effect lasts for 1d6 nights.                      | Dark circles form under the character's eyes, and they wake up in a cold sweat, haunted by vivid, terrifying dreams that leave them restless.                                                             |
| 8    | **Blight-Touched Eyes:** The character's eyes become blackened or veined with darkness. They gain darkvision up to 60 feet but have disadvantage on attack rolls in bright light for 1d6 weeks.                                          | The character's eyes turn pitch black or become veined with dark lines, giving them an eerie, otherworldly appearance.                                                                                    |
| 9    | **Fungal Growth:** Small patches of fungus begin to grow on the character's skin. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks but advantage on Constitution saving throws against poison for 1d6 days.                        | Tiny clusters of fungi sprout from the character's skin, especially around their arms and neck, giving off a faint, earthy odor.                                                                          |
| 10   | **Delusions of Grandeur:** The character believes they have gained immense power. They have advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks but must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw to avoid reckless behavior for 1d6 days.                  | The character stands taller, their posture exuding confidence and arrogance as they believe themselves to be invincible.                                                                                  |
| 11   | The character's hair falls out in clumps. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Performance) checks but advantage on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks for 1d4 weeks.                                                                     | Strands of hair begin to fall from the character's scalp, leaving them with noticeable bald patches and a more streamlined appearance.                                                                    |
| 12   | **Blighted Strength:** The character feels a surge of dark power. They gain +2 to Strength for 1d6 hours but suffer 1 level of exhaustion when the effect ends.                                                                          | Muscles bulge and veins darken as a surge of strength courses through the character, but the power comes with an ominous, draining cost.                                                                  |
| 13   | **Itching Sensation:** The character feels a constant itch under their skin. They have disadvantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks for 1d4 days.                                                                                       | The character constantly scratches and rubs their skin, distracted by an unbearable itching sensation beneath the surface.                                                                                |
| 14   | **Blighted Tongue:** The character's tongue turns black and swollen. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks but can speak and understand Abyssal for 1d6 days.                                                           | The character's tongue darkens and swells, making speech difficult but granting them an understanding of the dark language of Abyssal.                                                                    |
| 15   | **Nausea:** The character experiences bouts of nausea. They have disadvantage on Constitution saving throws and attack rolls for 1d4 days.                                                                                               | The character's face turns pale green as they frequently double over, clutching their stomach in discomfort.                                                                                              |
| 16   | **Blight-Fueled Rage:** The character flies into uncontrollable rages. They gain advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks and melee attack rolls but must make a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw to avoid attacking allies for 1d6 hours.      | The character's eyes blaze with fury, and they tremble with uncontrollable rage, lashing out at anyone nearby.                                                                                            |
| 17   | **Shadow Step:** The character can teleport up to 30 feet to a location they can see that is in dim light or darkness. They can use this ability once per long rest for 1d4 weeks.                                                       | The character momentarily fades into shadow before reappearing a short distance away, blending seamlessly into the darkness.                                                                              |
| 18   | **Skeletal Appearance:** The character becomes gaunt and skeletal. They have advantage on Intimidation checks but disadvantage on Constitution saving throws for 1d6 weeks                                                               | The character's features become sunken, and their skin stretches tightly over their bones, giving them a haunting, skeletal look.                                                                         |
| 19   | **Enhanced Senses:** The character gains heightened senses. They have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks but suffer disadvantage on saving throws against blinding or deafening effects for 1d6 days.                               | The character's eyes and ears become unusually sharp, their pupils dilating as they take in every detail around them.                                                                                     |
| 20   | **Blight Resilience:** The character becomes temporarily resistant to the blight. They gain resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on saving throws against disease for 1d6 days.                                                   | A faint, dark glow envelops the character, and they feel an unusual resilience against the blight's corrupting influence.                                                                                 |
