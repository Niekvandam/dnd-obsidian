# Blight overview

[[Helionis]] slowly but surely corrupts areas, where he turns normal gems into [[Blight Gems]]. If enough Blight gems are found in an area, they can manifest themselves into a [[Blight Entity]]. In the past, the blight has been banished by local druids, and shattered back into the crystals they came from. These entities can range from small foes to malevolent forces. Relative to the amount of [[Blight Gems]] in the area. 

Banished blight entities would be banished back into the crystals from once they came. Until slowly becoming corrupted again. Over time, these sapphires would be found by creatures, human and goblins alike. Some fascinated by their ability to communicate with the entity.

When someone touches a blight gem for the first time, a handful of different ways for the blight to manifest can occur. The table can be found in the [[Blight Gems]] Table.

## The blight's Goal
[[Helionis]] (corrupted by [[Umbraxis]]) seeks to corrupt and consume the natural world, spreading darkness and decay through its gems. The ultimate goal of the blight is to corrupt the world, and make it safe for the [[Umbraxis]] to set and reveal its true self. Only at night is the world able to see [[Helionis]] for what it is, a vessel for [[Umbraxis]] -- A monstrous deity. 

# Story progression
## Initial encounter
The player encounters [[Liora Moonshadow]], an elf from [[Mossfield]], who tells them about the Blight affecting nearby areas like [[Mossfield]] and [[Bramblebrook]]. 

Once they investigate the missing livestock, they will be lead to the [[Hidden cave system]], where they first encounter the Goblins and the [[Blight Gems]].

## Retreat
Overwhelmed by the goblins, the players are forced to retreat from the cave. They gather information and resources, preparing for a return. 

## The Herbalist's Circle
The players learn about [[The herbalist's Circle]] and their rituals. They seek guidance from [[Marigold Thistlefoot]] and [[Elder Bramble]], who provide historical insights and clues about the blight gems. 

## Investigation
### Return to the cave
Better prepared, the players return to the [[Hidden cave system]]. But once they return to the cave. The goblins have fled. Only leaving behind a handful of crystals. There are abandoned huts and tools scattered about. The only person that remains is [[Karpo]], the rogue fisherman, who is sstill passionately fishing by the underground lake. 

#### Investigation points
##### The goblin settlement
The players find the goblin encampment deserted. The campfires are cold and the livestock pens are empty. 

**Clues**:
- Abandoned Blight Gems:
	- A few blight gems are scattered around the ground. Some partially mined, others fully formed. 
- Rushed departure:
	- Signs of a hasty retreat are evident, with items strewn about and unfinished tasks all over the place. 
- Left instructions:
	- Among the remnants of the goblin settlements, the players find a crumpled piece of parchment with instructions written in a harsh, hurried script. 
		- Unknown author
		- Content: The goblins are instructed to relocate to a new, more secure location to continue their operations. The note mentions a ritual to enhance the blight gems further, and a meeting point in a distant, secluded area in the Lush Valley.
		- Symbols: The parchment bears symbols and sigils unfamiliar to the players. Possibly connected to the blight entity.




## Natural supporters and opponents

### Supporters
#### Goblins: 
The goblins in the [[Hidden cave system]] have been harvesting [[Blight Gems]] to perform rituals that strengthen their connection to the Blight entity. They believe that by serving the entity, they will gain power and protection, ensuring their survival and dominance.

#### [[Corrupted Druids]]: 
Some druids, seduced by the power of the Blight, have turned away from their sacred duties and now aid in spreading the curse. They perform dark rituals to enhance the Blight's reach.

### Opponents
[[The herbalist's Circle]]: 
[[Elder Bramble]] is one of the only people still alive that remember how a group of powerful druids banished a Blight entity, hundreds of years ago. He is well aware that a new age of blight could be upon us. 

[[Liora Moonshadow]]:
An elf ranger who has witnessed the devastation caused by the Blight in her homeland. She has come to Willowbrook seeking allies to help combat this spreading darkness. She has touched the blight, and is suffering from some effects.
