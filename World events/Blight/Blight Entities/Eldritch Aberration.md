
**Patron:** [[The Great Old One]] 
**Language:** #DeepSpeech 
**Magic Type:** #Illusion, #Enchantment, #Conjuration (Portals)

**Overview:** The Eldritch Aberration warps reality itself, distorting perceptions and causing madness. Its presence brings about unnatural weather, strange mutations, and reality-defying phenomena.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sky churns with ominous clouds, and the air hums with an unnatural energy. Creatures twist and writhe, their forms defying nature."
    - **Presence:** Erratic weather, mutated animals.
    - **Communication:** Whispered secrets and nonsensical phrases in Deep Speech.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players may experience sudden visions or hear maddening whispers.
- **Stage 2:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Reality itself seems to bend and shift. Trees grow sideways, and the ground seems to pulse with a life of its own. Strange visions haunt the mind."
    - **Presence:** Warped landscape, bizarre plant growth, hallucinations.
    - **Communication:** The Eldritch Aberration may speak through the twisted plants or in dreams.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players may find themselves slipping into alternate realities or facing their own nightmares.
- **Stage 3:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Portals to otherworldly realms tear through the fabric of reality. The landscape is a chaotic swirl of colors and shapes, defying all logic and reason."
    - **Presence:** Portals to other dimensions, shifting skies and ground.
    - **Communication:** Direct mental contact, causing confusion or offering cryptic guidance.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must close the portals or face creatures from other dimensions invading the region.
