**Patron:** [[The Celestial (Corrupted)]] 
**Language:** #TwistedCelestial 
**Magic Type:** #Transmutation (Plants), #Conjuration (Summoning Plant Creatures)

**Overview:** The Fallen Guardian twists nature, making it both beautiful and deadly. Its influence causes rampant plant growth that becomes hostile and sentient, turning the region into an overgrown and dangerous wilderness.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Vegetation runs rampant, turning paths into tangled thickets. The sky is an ever-shifting canvas of clouds and blinding light."
    - **Presence:** Rapid plant growth, tangled paths.
    - **Communication:** Whispers in the rustling leaves in Twisted Celestial.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might hear the Guardian's voice urging them to embrace the wild.
- **Stage 2:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "The plants awaken, their vines and thorns acting with malevolent intent. Every step is a battle against the choking greenery."
    - **Presence:** Sentient, hostile vegetation.
    - **Communication:** The Guardian speaks through the rustling of the leaves and the creaking of the trees.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might find themselves ensnared by vines that whisper of surrender.
- **Stage 3:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "An impenetrable jungle of living nightmares. Giant plant creatures patrol the overgrown ruins, and the light is blinding and relentless."
    - **Presence:** Overgrown jungle, giant plant creatures.
    - **Communication:** The Guardian can appear as a spectral figure in the flora, speaking directly.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must find and destroy the heart of the jungle to quell the Guardian's power.
