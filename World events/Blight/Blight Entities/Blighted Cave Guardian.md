**Description:** The Blighted Cave Guardian is a formidable beast, twisted by the blight into a monstrous form. It has bioluminescent patches on its skin that glow with a sickly green light, and its eyes burn with a malevolent intelligence. The creature's heart has been replaced by the Blighted Heartstone, making it the source of its power.
- **Abilities:**
    - **Necrotic Aura:** The creature emits an aura of necrotic energy that weakens those who come too close.
    - **Blighted Strike:** Its attacks inflict additional necrotic damage and have a chance to corrupt the target with the blight.
    - **Regeneration:** The blighted energy grants the creature limited regenerative abilities, making it hard to defeat.

## Location

### [[Hidden cave system]]
Can be found in a hidden chamber in the cave system, guarding a [[Heartstone]].
