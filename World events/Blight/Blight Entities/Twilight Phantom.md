**Patron:** [[The Celestial (Corrupted)]] 
**Language:** #Celestial and Haunting Echoes 
**Magic Type:** #Illusion, #Enchantment

**Overview:** The Twilight Phantom creates a realm of perpetual twilight, using its powers to bathe the land in a soothing yet eerie glow. While it may appear calm and peaceful, the unending twilight can be disorienting and hypnotic.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sun hangs low, casting the world in a perpetual twilight. Night blooms with activity, and the air is filled with a strange vibrancy."
    - **Presence:** Extended twilight, increased nocturnal activity.
    - **Communication:** The Twilight Phantom's voice can be heard in the gentle rustling of leaves and the soft hum of twilight.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might feel a calming presence, but prolonged exposure can cause lethargy and confusion.
- **Stage 2:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The world is awash in dim light, where plants glow softly and night creatures reign supreme. A mystical, otherworldly glow suffuses everything."
    - **Presence:** Perpetual twilight, bioluminescent plants and thriving nocturnal creatures.
    - **Communication:** The Phantom can speak through the glow of the plants and the light of the moon.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might find themselves entranced by the glow, losing track of time and direction.
- **Stage 3:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "An eternal twilight, where the land is illuminated by bioluminescent flora and fauna. The air is calm and serene, but the unending twilight can be disorienting."
    - **Presence:** Eternal twilight, magical bioluminescent creatures, and plants.
    - **Communication:** Direct mental contact with the Twilight Phantom, creating soothing yet deceptive visions.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must resist the hypnotic pull of the twilight and find a way to restore the natural day-night cycle.

**Abilities of Twilight Phantom:**

- **Ethereal Glide:** Moves silently and can pass through solid objects.
- **Twilight Aura:** Creates an area of dim light that confuses and weakens enemies.
- **Luminous Call:** Summons bioluminescent creatures to fight alongside it.
