**Patron:** [[The Genie (Efreet)]] 
**Language:** #Primordial 
**Magic Type:** #Evocation (Fire), #Transmutation (Earth)

**Overview:** The Corrupted Elemental turns the region into a fiery desert, using its elemental power to create an inhospitable environment. It thrives on heat and desolation, making the land a barren wasteland.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The heat is unrelenting, and the air carries the sting of sand. Water becomes scarce as the land dries and cracks."
    - **Presence:** Hot, dry air, frequent dust storms, drying water sources.
    - **Communication:** Roaring flames and crackling fire speak in Primordial, conveying rage and hunger.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might hear the elemental's voice in the crackling of fires, urging them to succumb to the heat.
- **Stage 2:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "Sandstorms rage across an arid wasteland. The flora and fauna struggle to survive in the relentless heat and choking dust."
    - **Presence:** Constant sandstorms, desert-like conditions, adapted or dying plants and animals.
    - **Communication:** The elemental's voice can be heard in the howling winds, promising power in exchange for servitude.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might find themselves overwhelmed by the heat and sand, needing to find shelter and water.
- **Stage 3:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "A desert of fire and sand, where only the fiercest creatures survive. The land is a sea of dunes under a burning sky."
    - **Presence:** Vast desert, scorching temperatures, fire elementals and fiery creatures roam.
    - **Communication:** The Corrupted Elemental can appear as a fiery apparition, speaking directly.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must find a way to cool the elemental's fury, perhaps by locating a hidden oasis or performing a cooling ritual.

**Abilities of Corrupted Elemental:**

- **Fire Blast:** Launches powerful fire attacks that incinerate enemies.
- **Sandstorm:** Creates blinding sandstorms that disorient and damage.
- **Heatwave:** Generates intense heat that weakens and exhausts foes.
