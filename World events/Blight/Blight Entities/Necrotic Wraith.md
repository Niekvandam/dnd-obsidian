**Patron:** [[The Undead]] **Language:** Infernal **Magic Type:** Necromancy, Illusion (Fear)

**Overview:** The Necrotic Wraith spreads death and decay, turning the region into a necropolis. Its power raises the dead and fills the air with the chill of the grave, making it a land of fog and shadows.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "A bone-chilling cold seeps into the bones, and a dense fog blankets the land. The stench of decay is pervasive, and the dead stir in their graves."
    - **Presence:** Cold, fog, rising undead.
    - **Communication:** Whispered threats and promises of power in Infernal.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players may hear the voices of the dead calling out to them.
- **Stage 2:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The fog grows so thick it feels like a living entity. Ghostly apparitions drift through the mists, their touch draining the warmth of life from all they encounter."
    - **Presence:** Thicker fog, more powerful undead.
    - **Communication:** The Necrotic Wraith can appear as a ghostly figure, speaking directly.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players may be haunted by persistent ghosts or see visions of their own deaths.
- **Stage 3:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "An endless army of the undead roams the land, turning it into a vast necropolis. The sky is a constant gray, and the living find no refuge here."
    - **Presence:** Necropolis with undead armies, overcast skies.
    - **Communication:** The wraith can possess corpses to deliver messages.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must perform a cleansing ritual to banish the wraith and its minions.
