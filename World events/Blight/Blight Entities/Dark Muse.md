### [[Dark Muse]]

**Patron:** [[The Hexblade]] 
**Language:** #Infernal and Whispers 
**Magic Type:** #Enchantment, #Illusion

**Overview:** The Dark Muse cloaks the land in shadows and fear, manipulating reality to create a realm of twilight and paranoia. It sows dread and confusion, making the very environment an enemy.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Shadows seem to reach out, and the air grows heavy with unseen dread. Tiny, impish figures flit just at the edge of sight."
    - **Presence:** Lengthened shadows, oppressive atmosphere, minor shadow creatures causing disturbances.
    - **Communication:** Whispers in Infernal and eerie murmurs that seem to come from the shadows themselves.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might hear voices that sow doubt and fear, seeing movement in the corners of their vision.
- **Stage 2:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Daylight is swallowed by darkness, and shadowy figures haunt every corner. A pervasive sense of dread grips the heart."
    - **Presence:** Deepened shadows, reduced visibility even in daylight, stalking shadowy figures.
    - **Communication:** The Dark Muse can project its voice through the shadows, offering deals or taunting the players.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might find themselves haunted by shadowy figures that follow them, whispering temptations or threats.
- **Stage 3:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "A realm of eternal twilight, where shadowy beasts rule and the very ground seems to conspire against you. Every step feels uncertain and treacherous."
    - **Presence:** Perpetual twilight, powerful shadow creatures dominate, landscape shifts and changes.
    - **Communication:** Direct mental contact with the Dark Muse, creating vivid hallucinations and twisted dreams.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must navigate an ever-changing landscape and confront their worst fears manifested by the Dark Muse.

**Abilities of Dark Muse:**

- **Shadow Step:** Teleports between shadows, making it hard to track or hit.
- **Whispering Fear:** Sends whispers that induce fear and paranoia.
- **Dark Embrace:** Envelops an area in darkness, blinding and disorienting those within.
