**Patron:** [[The Fathomless]] 
**Language:** #Aquan 
**Magic Type:** #Conjuration (Water), #Transmutation (Aquatic Creatures)

**Overview:** The Abyssal Leviathan brings the power of the deep seas to the surface, flooding the land and turning it into an aquatic nightmare. Its influence grows as the land becomes submerged and aquatic monsters reign.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Endless rain turns the land to mire, and streams swell into raging torrents. Fish and other aquatic creatures grow monstrous and hostile."
    - **Presence:** Heavy rains, flooding, aggressive aquatic life.
    - **Communication:** Murmurs and roars in Aquan from the depths.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might hear the Leviathan's call in the sound of the rain.
- **Stage 2:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "The land is swallowed by water, transforming fields into swamps. The roar of monstrous beasts echoes through the perpetual mist."
    - **Presence:** Permanent flooding, frequent aquatic monster attacks.
    - **Communication:** The Leviathan can appear as a shadow in the water, speaking in Aquan.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players might be lured by the Leviathan's promises of power if they accept its embrace.
- **Stage 3:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "An underwater realm of darkness and predation. The drowned lands are ruled by vicious sea creatures, and pockets of air are precious and rare."
    - **Presence:** Completely submerged lands, dominant sea predators.
    - **Communication:** Direct telepathic communication with promises or threats.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players must find ancient underwater relics to seal the Leviathan's power.
