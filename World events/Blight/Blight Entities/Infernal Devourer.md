**Patron:** [[The Fiend]]
**Language:** Abyssal 
**Magic Type:** #Evocation ( #Fire), #Conjuration (Summoning Infernal Creatures)

**Overview:** The Infernal Devourer is a malevolent entity that thrives on destruction and chaos, spreading fire and devastation wherever it goes. As it advances through its stages, the land it occupies becomes a hellscape of scorched earth, boiling rivers, and constant infernal storms.

**Effect on Region:**

- **Stage 1:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The air crackles with heat as the ground begins to split. Ash drifts like dark snowflakes, and the once-green fields smolder under an oppressive heat."
    - **Presence:** Scorched earth and occasional small fires; animals become aggressive.
    - **Communication:** Hisses and whispers in Abyssal can be heard in the crackling of fires.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Players may see fiery visions or hear voices urging them towards acts of violence.
- **Stage 2:**
    
    - **Flavour Text:** "Thick smoke fills the lungs as flames dance across the landscape. The screams of infernal creatures echo through the choking haze, a symphony of chaos and destruction."
    - **Presence:** Thick smoke, withered plants, infernal creatures appear.
    - **Communication:** The Infernal Devourer may speak directly through flames, offering deals or taunts.
    - **Possible Interactions:** Infernal creatures may approach the players with offers of power in exchange for loyalty.
- **Stage 3:**
    - **Flavour Text:** "The sun is blotted out by a sky of roiling smoke. Rivers of molten lava carve new paths through the land, and the very air burns with a hellish intensity."
    - **Presence:** Lava-filled wastelands, constant heat and fire.
    - **Communication:** Fiery portals open, revealing glimpses of an infernal dimension.
    - **Possible Interactions:** The players may need to perform a purification ritual to close the portals and banish the entity.
