#### **Journal Entries**

**Entry 1: The Commission** _"They came to me in the dead of night, cloaked figures, faces hidden. They spoke in whispers and asked no questions of me. They handed me a stone, unlike any I’ve seen—pale and faintly pulsing as though it had a heartbeat of its own. I was tasked with embedding this gem into a pendant, finely crafted, though I know not why. They would not reveal their purpose, only that this was for the survival of Willowbrook. I should have refused, but the fear in their voices... it chilled me."_
- **Hint:** The cloaked figures were members of the secretive [[The herbalist's Circle]], but the blacksmith did not know their identities. They entrusted him with a [[Heartstone]], though he knew it by no such name.

**Entry 2: The Pendant** _"It took me a week to craft it—fine silver, woven like the roots of a tree, with the stone nestled in the centre. The gem thrummed as I worked, and sometimes it seemed as though the silver bent of its own accord, drawn to the stone’s power. When I handed it to them, they said little and offered even less by way of thanks. But I could not let my curiosity be buried with the silver I worked. I had to know what it was for."_

- **Hint:** The pendant was designed to channel the power of the [[Heartstone]], though the blacksmith didn’t understand its full significance. The design, resembling tree roots, hints at the [[The Whispering Willow]].

**Entry 3: The Ritual** _"I followed them that night, staying far enough behind to remain unseen. They gathered in the square beneath the **[[The Whispering Willow]]**, the ancient tree older than any story. They stood in a circle, and in the middle, one of them held the pendant aloft. A glow, faint at first, began to spread from the stone. The whispers grew—though no mouths moved—and the tree itself seemed to lean toward the pendant. Something was happening. Something I do not understand to this day."_

- **Hint:** The **Herbalist’s Circle** was performing the [[The Guardian's Blessing]], using the pendant to channel the power of the **Whispering Willow** and banish the unseen **Blight Entity** that had been plaguing the region.

**Entry 4: The Disappearance** _"The days that followed were strange. The lands that had turned grey, where crops had died and animals refused to graze, slowly began to heal. The air grew less heavy, and the sky less dark. The sickness that had plagued the village began to lift. It wasn’t overnight, but in time, the lands around Willowbrook were restored. No one spoke of it, and those cloaked figures vanished as quietly as they had come."_

- **Hint:** The gradual restoration of the land after the **Blight Entity** was banished points to the success of the **Herbalist’s Circle** and the **Guardian’s Blessing** ritual. However, the blacksmith’s ancestor remained unaware of the true nature of the ritual or the threat.

**Entry 5: The Warning** _"I don’t know if they will return, or if the pendant will ever be used again. But something tells me that the quiet will not last forever. I leave this journal as a record, should Willowbrook ever need to remember the days when the land was dying and something darker walked among us. May those who find this learn from what we did not understand."_

- **Hint:** The ancestor left this journal as a warning to future generations, sensing that the **Blight** might one day return and that the ritual—and the pendant—may be needed again. The journal’s final words suggest that Borin’s ancestor had an inkling that this was not the last time Willowbrook would face such darkness.
