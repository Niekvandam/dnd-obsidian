
## Quests

### Herbalist's Errand

**Description:** Marigold needs rare herbs from the Whispering Woods for her potions. She asks the players to gather these herbs while avoiding the strange lights.
- **Location:** The Whispering Woods
- **Reward:** Healing potions, herbal remedies that grant temporary bonuses (e.g., +1 to a stat for 1 hour).
- **Moonlight Petals:** Flowers that bloom under the light of the full moon, symbolizing the essence of [[Lunaris]].
