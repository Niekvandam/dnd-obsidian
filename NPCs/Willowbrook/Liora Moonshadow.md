# Liora Moonshadow

## Overview
An enigmatic elf ranger who arrives seeking aid for her blighted homeland. She carries tales of a spreading darkness and hints at a larger, looming threat. Her arrival marks the beginning of the players' journey beyond [[Willowbrook]].

## Appearance
- **Age:** Appears to be in her early 100s (young for an elf)
- **Features:** Tall and lithe, with silver hair cascading down her back, however, it appears that she is balding. and piercing blue eyes that seem to see into the soul. She has an ethereal quality, almost as if she's part of the forest itself.
- **Attire:** Worn traveler's cloak made of dark green fabric, leather armor adorned with elven motifs, a bow slung over her shoulder, and a quiver of finely crafted arrows.
## Personality
- **Traits:** Mysterious, determined, observant
- **Quirks:** Speaks in a melodic tone, often pauses as if listening to the forest around her, has a shadowy aura that makes her presence both intriguing and unsettling.
## Skills and Abilities
- **Primary Skill:** Expert Tracker (can follow tracks and signs that others would miss)
- **Secondary Skills:** Skilled Archer (proficient with the bow), Darkness Sense (can sense the presence of dark forces nearby)

- **Blighted**: She is suffering from the touch of the blight. She has disadvantage on Charisma/performance checks, but Advantage on Dexterity checks for 3 weeks. 

## Relationships
- **Mysterious Traveler:** Liora Moonshadow is the [[Mysterious Traveler]]
- **[[Finnian Greenbriar]]:** Takes an interest in the young apprentice's curiosity, seeing potential in him.
- **[[Marigold Thistlefoot]]:** Shares knowledge of nature and the blight, forming a bond over their mutual understanding of natural disturbances.

## Plot Hooks
- Introduces the larger threat of the spreading blight that affects her homeland and now encroaches on Willowbrook.
- Can guide players through dangerous territories, using her knowledge of the wilds and her tracking abilities.
- Reveals hints of an ancient evil awakening, connected to the disturbances in Willowbrook and beyond, leading the players on a quest to uncover and combat this looming threat.
