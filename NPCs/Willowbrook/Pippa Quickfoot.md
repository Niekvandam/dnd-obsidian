**Lives in**: [[The Resting Oak Inn]]
### Pippa Quickfoot - NPC Overview

**Race:** Halfling  
**Gender:** Female  
**Occupation:** Innkeeper and Tavern Owner of _The Resting Oak_ in Willowbrook  
**Age:** Mid-40s  
**Alignment:** Neutral Good  
**Personality:** Energetic, talkative, warm-hearted

### Description

Pippa Quickfoot is a cheerful and welcoming halfling who runs _The Resting Oak_, the village’s only inn and tavern. Standing just over 3 feet tall, she’s a bundle of energy, always bustling around serving drinks, chatting with the locals, and making sure everyone feels at home. With her sharp, intelligent eyes and infectious smile, she has a way of making even the grumpiest of travelers feel welcome. Her dark, curly hair is often tied back with a colorful scarf, and she’s known for wearing practical but brightly colored aprons covered in flour and smudges from her cooking.

**Appearance:**  
Pippa is small even for a halfling, with a round face, rosy cheeks, and a constant twinkle in her eyes. She moves with quick, light steps, earning her the name "Quickfoot." Her attire is always practical but with a flair for bright colors and patterns. She often hums to herself as she works, filling the inn with her cheerful presence.

### Personality

Pippa is known for her vibrant personality. She’s incredibly friendly, loves to talk, and can be a bit nosy, but her heart is always in the right place. She is the go-to person in Willowbrook for local gossip and rumors, as the village’s residents trust her and love to chat with her during their visits to the tavern. Despite her nosiness, Pippa is discreet and will only share information with those she trusts or believes need to know.

- **Loyal and Protective:** Pippa is fiercely loyal to her regular patrons and considers the villagers her extended family. She’s protective of Willowbrook and will quickly come to someone’s defense if they’re in danger or being unfairly treated.
- **Nosy but Discreet:** While she loves to eavesdrop and gather information, she’s careful about what she shares and with whom. She knows when to keep a secret and often uses her knowledge to help others in subtle ways.
- **Quick-Witted and Playful:** Pippa enjoys playful banter, especially with adventurers who pass through her tavern. She’s sharp and can hold her own in any conversation, often surprising people with her wit.

**Quirks:**

- She’s known for always having a cup of tea or a baked treat in her hand.
- Pippa has an uncanny ability to remember names, faces, and personal details about everyone who stays at the inn.
- She hums old folk songs from her childhood, and patrons often find themselves unknowingly humming along with her.

### Roleplaying Pippa Quickfoot

- **Voice:** Pippa’s voice is bright, quick, and full of warmth. She speaks in rapid bursts and often peppers her speech with terms of endearment like "dear" or "love." She’s always upbeat, even in stressful situations.
- **Behavior:** She’s constantly moving around, checking on her guests, and making sure everyone is comfortable. She gestures a lot when she talks and has a habit of touching people lightly on the arm or shoulder when speaking to them, making her feel approachable and friendly.
- **Interactions with Players:** Pippa loves adventurers and will be eager to hear about their travels. She’s likely to offer them a room for a reduced rate or on credit if they’re low on funds, trusting that they’ll pay her back eventually. She’s also a good source of rumors and information, though players might have to spend time in the tavern talking to her or buying a few rounds for the locals to get the juiciest details.

### Dialogue Samples

- **Greeting:**  
    “Well, look who’s come in from the cold! Welcome to _The Resting Oak_! Find a seat, and I’ll be right with you, love.”

- **Offering Information (Subtle):**  
    "Oh, I wouldn’t say I _know_ much about that strange traveler who came through... but, well, folks do talk when they’ve had a few drinks, don’t they?"
    
- **Concerned for Willowbrook:**  
    "Things have been feeling a bit off lately, don’t you think? It’s the quiet, really... quieter than usual. Makes me wonder if something’s brewing out in those woods."

### Plot Hooks for Pippa

- **Village Gossip:** Pippa could provide clues or rumors about the strange lights in the forest, the hidden cave system, or missing livestock. She hears more than she lets on and may share information if she trusts the players.
- **Personal Quest:** Pippa is worried about one of her regulars who hasn’t shown up in a few days. She may ask the players to check on them, leading to a potential side quest.
- **Tavern Rumors:** Pippa hears travelers talking about a hidden treasure in the area. She could casually mention it to the players, sparking a treasure hunt or exploration quest.

### Key Relationships

- **Elder Bramble:** She deeply respects Elder Bramble, often turning to her for advice on village matters. Though she occasionally feels that Elder Bramble keeps too many secrets, she trusts her judgment.
- **Thorn Battlehammer:** Pippa enjoys bantering with Thorn and appreciates his gruff but good-hearted nature. She’ll often share drinks with him after a long day’s work.
- **Marigold Thistlefoot:** Pippa admires Marigold’s herbal knowledge and occasionally asks her for remedies to soothe the aches and pains of running a busy tavern.

### Secrets and Rumors

- Pippa has secretly stashed away a collection of letters from an old flame who was a traveling bard. She hides them in the rafters of _The Resting Oak_, and no one knows about her past romantic life.
- She’s heard some strange things about the Whispering Willow and may have seen shadowy figures (the Herbalist's Circle) gathering there late at night, but she’s too afraid to investigate further.
