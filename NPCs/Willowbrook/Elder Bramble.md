## Overview
Elder Bramble is the wise and venerable leader of [[Willowbrook]]. Having seen many seasons, she possesses an in-depth understanding of the land's rhythms and the village's history.

## Appearance
- **Age:** Elderly
- **Features:** Sharp eyes, silver hair, slightly hunched posture
- **Attire:** Simple, earthy-toned robes with intricate embroidery symbolizing local flora

## Personality
- **Traits:** Wise, calm, perceptive
- **Quirks:** Often found with a cup of herbal tea, speaks in riddles occasionally

## Skills and Abilities
- **Primary Skill:** Lorekeeper (Extensive knowledge of local history and lore)
- **Secondary Skills:** Insightful (Can sense when something is amiss and often provides guidance)

## Relationships
- **[[Marigold Thistlefoot]]:** Often consults her for herbal remedies and natural disturbances
- **[[Thorn Battlehammer]]:** Trusts his judgment in matters of village defense
- **Pippa Quickfoot:** Receives updates on village happenings

## Plot Hooks
- Holds knowledge about the strange lights and the [[Hidden cave system]]. Is aware that Goblins might be the culprit. 
	- Somehow, during the evening hours, he has seen the goblins walk.
- May send the players on quests to uncover ancient village secrets
- Can provide historical context and lore that hints at future adventures
- Is suspicious of [[Liora Moonshadow]], and thinks that she is a [[Mysterious Traveler]]

- Knows something about the '[[The Blight]]', but refuses to believe that it still exists. Doesn't know much about it. 
- Has heard rumors of [[Places/Sunset/Cities/The Sunken Citadel/The Hidden Order of Luminis]], but doesn't know anything about them and doesn't talk about it if he isn't bribed (DC 18)
