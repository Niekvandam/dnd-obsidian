### Garrik's Secret Mission

Garrik the Woodcutter, resided in the [[Abandoned tower]] before succumbing to madness, had a deep but secret connection to the ancient Whispering Willow and the legend of [[The Guardian's Blessing]]. Although he was not a member of [[The herbalist's Circle]], he discovered the protective powers of the Whispering Willow on his own. Motivated by a desire to protect his village from the blight, Garrik began secretly crafting wooden figurines imbued with the tree's magic.

### Wooden Figurines

- **Material:** The wooden figurines found in the hidden compartment of the tower are carved from the wood of the Whispering Willow itself. This wood is imbued with protective magic.

- **Purpose:** Each figurine represents a villager and was created to offer them protection from the blight. Garrik, guided by whispers from the tree and his own intuition, crafted these figures to shield the villagers from harm.
