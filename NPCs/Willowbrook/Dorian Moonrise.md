#### Basic Information
- **Name:** Dorian Moonrise
- **Age:** 68
- **Class:** [[Night-casters]] (5th level Illusionist)
- **Race:** Human
- **Background:** Sage

#### Appearance
- **Height:** 5'10"
- **Build:** Lean and wiry
- **Hair:** Long, silver hair often tied back in a loose ponytail
- **Eyes:** Deep blue, reflecting the starry night sky
- **Clothing:** Wears dark, flowing robes adorned with silver embroidery depicting constellations and celestial symbols. He carries an ornate staff with a glowing crystal at its tip, used as both a focus and a walking aid.

#### Personality
- **Traits:** Wise, patient, and contemplative. Dorian is known for his calming presence and deep well of knowledge.
- **Quirks:** Often speaks in metaphors related to the night sky and celestial bodies. Has a habit of stargazing, even during the twilight.
- **Ideals:** Balance and harmony are essential for a peaceful life. Knowledge is the key to maintaining this balance.
- **Bonds:** Deeply committed to the well-being of Willowbrook and its residents. He views the Whispering Willow as a sacred symbol and source of inspiration.
- **Flaws:** Can be overly cautious and slow to act. Sometimes loses himself in contemplation and forgets the urgency of immediate dangers.

#### Backstory
Dorian Moonrise has lived in Willowbrook for the last 40 years, serving as the village's night-caster and wise elder. Born into a family of scholars in a distant city of the [[Iron Fortress]], Dorian showed an early affinity for illusion magic and celestial lore. His parents, recognizing his potential, sent him to study under a master illusionist who introduced him to the art of night-casting.

After completing his apprenticeship, Dorian traveled across Sunset, seeking ancient knowledge and forgotten lore about the twin suns, [[Helionis]] and [[Lunaris]]. His journey eventually led him to Willowbrook, where he found a community in need of his unique skills. Dorian decided to settle in the village, dedicating himself to the protection and well-being of its residents.

For decades, Dorian has maintained the nightly dome of darkness, allowing the people of Willowbrook to experience rest and tranquility. He has also become a trusted advisor to [[Elder Bramble]] and a mentor to young aspiring night-casters.

#### Special Abilities
- **Create Night Dome:** As an action, Dorian can create a dome of night that covers a 100-foot radius, lasting for 8 hours. Within the dome, the area is treated as nighttime, complete with a starry sky and a phantom moon. This requires concentration.
- **Soothe Mind:** Dorian can cast the Calm Emotions spell without expending a spell slot once per long rest.
- **Illusory Defense:** Dorian gains the ability to cast Mirror Image as a reaction when targeted by an attack, once per long rest.
- **Aura of Tranquility:** Allies within the night dome gain advantage on Wisdom saving throws and recover from exhaustion at twice the normal rate.

## Night-casting
### Setting the Scene
As dusk begins to settle over the village of Willowbrook, the villagers gather in the town square, awaiting the nightly ritual performed by the esteemed night-caster, [[Dorian Moonrise]]. The air is filled with anticipation and a sense of calm as the twilight sky casts a gentle glow over the surroundings.

#### Steps of the Ritual
1. **Preparation and Gathering:**
- **Description:** Dorian steps into the center of the square, carrying a staff adorned with glowing crystals and wrapped in ivy. The villagers form a respectful circle around him, whispering in hushed tones.
- **Atmosphere:** The faint sound of a nearby stream and the rustling of leaves add to the tranquil ambiance. Lanterns are dimmed, and an expectant hush falls over the crowd.
2. . **Invocation of the Night:**
- **Description:** Dorian raises his staff high, and with a resonant voice, begins to chant an ancient incantation. The words are a melodic blend of Elvish and Draconic, resonating with the magic of the land.
- **Chant:** “Lumus noctem, umbrae pacem. Astra somnia, serenitatem duc. Helionis, cedat; Lunaris, surge.”
- **Atmosphere:** As he chants, the crystals on his staff begin to glow brighter, casting a soft, soothing light that envelops the square.
 3. **Gestures and Casting:**
- **Description:** Dorian performs a series of intricate hand movements, tracing symbols in the air that shimmer and leave trails of light. He gestures towards the sky, and slowly, a dome of darkness begins to form, spreading outwards to cover the village.
- **Gestures:** Sweeping arcs with the staff, precise hand movements, and a final, sweeping motion that draws the dome over the village.
- **Atmosphere:** The sky above darkens, stars begin to twinkle into existence, and a phantom moon rises, bathing the village in a gentle, silvery light.
4.  **Final Incantation:**
- **Description:** With the dome now fully formed, Dorian lowers his staff and speaks the final words of the incantation, sealing the illusion of night.
- **Chant:** “Noctis amplexus, tranquillitatis. Dormite secure, umbrae custodientes.”
- **Atmosphere:** The air seems to hum with magic, and a sense of peace settles over the village. The illusion of night is perfect, with constellations slowly moving across the sky and the soft chirping of nocturnal creatures.
5.  **Completion and Reflection:**
- **Description:** Dorian takes a moment to ensure the dome is stable, then lowers his staff and turns to the villagers with a serene smile. The villagers murmur their gratitude, and the night-caster steps aside to allow the night to take its course.
- **Atmosphere:** The village is now enveloped in a calming night, the artificial stars twinkling above. The villagers begin to disperse, heading to their homes for a restful sleep under the protective dome.

### Enhancing the Experience
- **Sound Effects:** Describe the subtle sounds accompanying the ritual, such as the hum of magic, the rustling of leaves, and the faint whisper of the night-caster’s chants.
- **Visuals:** Emphasize the visual beauty of the night dome, with stars and a phantom moon that look almost real. Mention the way the light shifts and changes as the dome forms.
- **Emotional Impact:** Convey the sense of peace and safety that the night dome brings to the villagers. Highlight how the ritual is a cherished part of their daily lives, creating a sense of community and continuity.
- **Player Interaction:** If the players are involved, they might be asked to assist in minor ways, such as lighting lanterns, guiding villagers, or even participating in the chant if they have relevant skills or magic.
