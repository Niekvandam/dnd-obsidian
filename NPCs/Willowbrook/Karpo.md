# Overview
Karpo is a small but friendly rogue who has taken refuge in the [[Hidden cave system]]. He is known for his skill in fishing and his cautious nature. Over the past few weeks, Karpo has been inadvertently exposed to the blight due to the goblins' activities. Causing some changes in his appearance and behaviour. 
## Appearance

Age: mid-30s
Height: 5'4
Build: Lean and wiry
Features: Sharp, intelligent eyes, a scruffy beard, pale skin with fungal growth here and there
- Small patches of fungus begin to grow on the character's skin. They have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks but advantage on Constitution saving throws against poison for 4 days
Attire: Wears black rags that blend into the shadows. His hat also functions as a cape, providing some protection and a bit of flair. 
![[Karpo.png]]
## Personality
- **Traits**: Friendly, cautious, somewhat paranoid
- **Quirks**: Often mutters to himself, particularly about the 'cursed goblins'. Has an unusual attachment to his fishing pole, which he has named "Lucky"

## Skills and abililties
- **Primary Skill:** Stealth and hiding (Dexterity)
- **Secondary Skills:** Fishing (Wisdom), trap-setting (Dexterity)
- **Blight Effect:** Minor hallucinations and a slightly feverish state due to prolonged exposure to blight. Occasionally sees shadows moving at the edge of his vision, making him more jittery and paranoid.


## Backstory

Karpo can be found near the underground lake in the Hidden Cave System. He has set up a small, hidden encampment among the rocks at the top of the lake, where he spends most of his time fishing and avoiding goblin patrols.

#### Interactions with Players

- **Initial Meeting:** The players may spot Karpo's fishing line hanging from a ledge. If they investigate, they can find him in his hidden spot.
- **Dialogue Options:**
   - **Fishing Enthusiast:** Karpo is initially wary but warms up if the players show interest in his fishing or share some food with him.
   - **Witness Account:** Karpo can provide valuable information about the goblins' activities, their numbers, and the layout of the cave system. However, he may require some reassurance or a small favor (like helping him with a minor task) before sharing his knowledge.
   - **Quest Hook:** Karpo might ask the players to help him retrieve a prized fishing lure that was stolen by the goblins. In return, he offers to guide them through a secret passage he knows of, which could bypass some of the goblin defenses.

# Communications
### General Mutterings

- "Can't trust the shadows... never know what's lurking."
- "Blast those goblins... always sneaking about."
- "If only they'd leave me in peace... just me and Lucky."
- "Fish are smarter than goblins, I tell ya... at least they don't dig up cursed gems."
- "Gotta stay quiet... quiet as a mouse... no, quieter than that."

### Reactions to Players

- **Surprised Reaction:** "By the gods! Where'd you come from? Thought I was the only one down here."
- **Suspicious Reaction:** "You ain't working for the goblins, are ya? Can't be too careful these days."
- **Relieved Reaction:** "Phew, you don't look like goblins. Got a friendly face, at least."
- **Cautious Reaction:** "You didn't touch those gems, did ya? Nasty things, they are. Best keep your distance."

### On the Goblins

- "Saw a whole pack of 'em just yesterday, carrying those blasted gems like they're treasure."
- "Goblins think they're so clever, setting traps everywhere. I've got my own ways around them."
- "They’re scared too, you know. Scared of whatever's in those gems. But they're greedy, and greed's a powerful thing."

### Fishing and Life in the Cave

- "Caught a big one last week... almost thought it was a goblin at first!"
- "Been here for weeks now. Lost track of time, but Lucky keeps me company."
- "Fishing's good here... better than you'd think for a cave. Must be something in the water."

### Blight Effects

- "Eyes play tricks on you down here... shadows move when they shouldn't."
- "Feel a fever coming on sometimes... like the blight's trying to get inside my head."
- "Gotta keep the mind clear... fishin' helps. Keeps the blight at bay, or so I hope."

### Offering Help

- "If you’re lookin' to move around without those goblins noticing, I know a few spots."
- "You seem like decent folk. Maybe we can help each other out. Got something I need, and you look like you can handle yourselves."
- "There's a passage I know, hidden from those goblins. Could get you deeper into the cave, if that's where you're headed."

### Sharing Information

- "Saw the goblins with some kind of map... might be worth lookin' for if you plan on sticking around."
- "Heard them talking about a big ritual... something about making the gems stronger. Didn't like the sound of it one bit."
- "They got a leader, a nasty piece of work. If you’re planning to take 'em on, that's who you want to watch out for."

### Farewell

- "Stay safe out there. And if you catch anything good, save a piece for old Karpo, eh?"
- "Don't let the blight get to you. Keep your wits about you, and you'll be fine."
- "Remember, the shadows ain't always what they seem. Keep your eyes open."
