## Overview

The **Hidden Order of Luminis** is a secretive faction that is aware of the existence and location of the Sunken Citadel. This clandestine group has guarded the secrets of the citadel for generations, operating in the shadows to protect the ancient knowledge and power contained within.

## Name and Purpose

- **Name:** The Hidden Order of Luminis
- **Purpose:** To safeguard the secrets and powerful artifacts of the Sunken Citadel, and to ensure that the knowledge and power of the [[Solar Core]] do not fall into the wrong hands.

## Reasons for Secrecy

The Hidden Order of Luminis believes that revealing the existence of the Sunken Citadel to the public would lead to chaos and disaster. They fear that:

1. **Power-Hungry Individuals:** Unscrupulous individuals or factions might seek to exploit the Solar Core and other powerful artifacts for their own gain, leading to further corruption and conflict.
2. **Blight Amplification:** The citadel's secrets, if misused, could amplify the blight's spread or create new threats, endangering the entire world.
3. **Protecting the Solar Core:** The Solar Core is a critical piece in the balance of power against [[Helionis]] and [[Umbraxis]]. If it were to be tampered with, the consequences could be catastrophic.

## Motives

The Hidden Order of Luminis is driven by a mix of altruistic and pragmatic motives:

1. **Preservation of Knowledge:** They aim to preserve the ancient knowledge and prevent it from being lost or corrupted.
2. **Protection of the World:** They see themselves as guardians of the world's balance, protecting it from the misuse of the citadel's power.
3. **Hidden Power:** Some members may be motivated by a desire to maintain control over powerful secrets and artifacts, ensuring that their own influence remains strong.

## Composition and Structure

The Hidden Order of Luminis is composed of a diverse group of individuals:

- **Scholars and Sages:** Experts in ancient lore, magic, and the history of the twin suns.
- **Warriors and Guardians:** Skilled fighters who protect the order's secrets and enforce its directives.
- **Rogues and Spies:** Masters of stealth and subterfuge who gather information and ensure the order's activities remain hidden.
- **Mystics and Seers:** Individuals with prophetic abilities who guide the order's actions and interpret signs and omens.

## Interaction with Others

The Hidden Order of Luminis rarely interacts openly with other groups, preferring to operate behind the scenes. However, they do engage in covert activities to influence events and protect their secrets:

1. **Agents and Informants:** They place agents and informants in key positions within other factions and communities to monitor threats and gather intelligence.
2. **Secret Allies:** They occasionally form alliances with individuals or groups that share their goals, providing aid and information without revealing their true identity.
3. **Guardians of Relics:** They secretly protect ancient sites and artifacts related to the Sunken Citadel, ensuring they are not disturbed or discovered.

## Notable Members

- **[[High Sage Elarion]]:** The current leader of the order, a wise and powerful mage with extensive knowledge of the citadel's history.
- **[[Captain Thalindra]]:** A skilled warrior who leads the order's guardians and enforcers, ensuring the protection of their secrets.
- **[[Whisperer Nyla]]:** A rogue who specializes in espionage and intelligence gathering, keeping the order informed of potential threats.
- **[[Seer Arion]]:** A mystic with the gift of prophecy, guiding the order's decisions through visions and omens.
