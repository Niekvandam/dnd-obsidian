## The Sunken Citadel / The Citadel of Luminae

### Overview
The **Citadel of Luminae**, now known as the **Sunken Citadel**, was once a grand and majestic stronghold of light and knowledge. It was the heart of an ancient civilization that thrived under the twin suns, [[Helionis]] and [[Lunaris]]. The citadel was a beacon of hope and protection, housing the powerful [[Solar Core]] which nullified the corrupting influence of [[Helionis]] after its fall to [[Umbraxis]].

### Location
The Citadel of Luminae was situated in the central highlands of Sunset, strategically placed to harness the energy of both suns. After the [[The Sundering]], it sank into the depths of the earth, becoming a forgotten legend buried beneath layers of rock and time.

### Inhabitants
- **Mages and Scholars:** The citadel was primarily inhabited by a group of mages and scholars dedicated to studying the celestial forces and protecting the world from corruption.
- **Guardians and Warriors:** A formidable group of guardians and warriors were stationed at Luminae to defend against external threats and to protect the [[Solar Core]].
- **Common Inhabitants:** Craftsmen, traders, and families also lived within the citadel, contributing to its vibrant community life.

### Major Historical Events

#### The Time of Twin Suns
Originally, the world of Sunset had two suns, [[Helionis]] and [[Lunaris]]. [[Helionis]] was the larger and brighter of the two, governing the day whilst providing vibrant energy for growth and activity. [[Lunaris]] was the smaller and gentler sun, governing the night. It provided a calm influence, fostering rest and reflection.

#### The Corruption
During its cosmic journey, the world of Sunset encountered [[Umbraxis]], a dark deity that corrupted the bigger of the two suns, [[Helionis]]. This left Helionis corrupted and forever changed. Its light, once pure and life-giving, became a source of blight. The corruption of Helionis had profound effects on its twin [[Lunaris]].

#### The Fall of Lunaris
As [[Helionis]]'s corruption spread, it began to consume its twin, [[Lunaris]]. The delicate balance that had existed between the two suns was shattered. The harmonious light of Lunaris was gradually overpowered by the blighted light of Helionis, creating [[Blight Gems]] and [[Blight Entities]] wherever the rays managed to go.

During this event, Lunaris attempted to resist the corruption, in what was known as [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]]. In a final, desperate act, Lunaris channeled all its remaining energy to push Helionis away, creating a momentary rift in the cosmos. After this event, Helionis became the sole sun, its corrupted light now dominating the skies of Sunset.

#### The Age of Blight
This event was closely monitored by a group of mages in the [[The Sunken Citadel|Citadel of Luminae]], where [[Lunaris’s Last Stand]] was dubbed [[Lunaris’s Last Stand|The Sundering]]. The mages in Luminae managed to harvest some of the power exerted by [[Lunaris]]. A group of researchers, led by [[Archmage P]], converted the harvested power of Lunaris into a core that could nullify some of Helionis's influence. This core, known as the [[Solar Core]], was placed at the center of the Citadel of Luminae. Beyond nullifying the rays of Helionis, it even managed to weaken the influence of [[Umbraxis]] on it, although further research was never achieved.

#### The Fall of Luminae
Throughout the years, the power of the [[Solar Core]] became known and drew a group of suspicious actors who tried to harvest power from the core. After creating a set of legendary weaponry and stashing it away in something only known as "[[The Golden Vault]]", the core became unstable. This unleashed a chain reaction of catastrophes, resulting in the city of Luminae sinking into the depths. This event marked the end of the ancient civilization and the beginning of Luminae's descent into legend.

### The Present Day
#### State of the Citadel

The **Sunken Citadel**, now lies buried deep beneath the earth, shrouded in darkness and decay. The grand halls and chambers are filled with the remnants of its former glory, but they are also tainted by the blight of [[Helionis]].

- **Structural Integrity:** Many parts of the citadel have crumbled and collapsed, creating dangerous pathways and hidden pitfalls. However, some areas remain remarkably intact, protected by ancient wards and enchantments.
- **Blight Corruption:** The pervasive influence of the blight has twisted and corrupted parts of the citadel, making it a treacherous place filled with blight-infested creatures and dark magic.

#### Relics and Artifacts

The Sunken Citadel is a treasure trove of ancient relics and powerful artifacts, remnants of the advanced civilization that once thrived there.

- **Ancient Tomes:** Books and scrolls containing forgotten knowledge, spells, and the history of [[Helionis]], [[Lunaris]], and the [[Solar Core]].
- **Magical Artifacts:** Powerful items created by the mages of Luminae, some of which are stored in the fabled [[The Golden Vault]].
- **Protective Wards:** Enchanted objects that still hold the power to repel the blight and protect the wielder from its effects.

#### Knowledge of its Location

The exact location of the Sunken Citadel has been lost to time, becoming the stuff of legend and myth. However, some clues and fragmented maps exist, guarded by ancient orders and hidden in obscure libraries.

- **Forgotten Maps:** Pieces of ancient maps that, when combined, reveal the path to the citadel.
- **Descendant Knowledge:** A few secretive groups, descendants of the original inhabitants, possess partial knowledge of the citadel's location and purpose.
- **Prophetic Visions:** Some seers and mystics have visions of the Sunken Citadel, providing cryptic clues to its whereabouts.

#### Inhabitants

While most of the original inhabitants perished or fled during the [[The Sundering]], some have survived through extraordinary means.

- **Ethereal Guardians:** Spirits of the ancient guardians and mages, bound to protect the citadel even in death. They can be both allies and adversaries to those who enter.
- **Blight-infested Creatures:** Corrupted beings that roam the citadel, mutated by the blight's influence.
- **Survivors:** A few individuals who have managed to survive in the citadel's depths, using powerful magic to sustain themselves. They may possess crucial knowledge and aid.
- **[[Places/Sunset/Cities/The Sunken Citadel/The Hidden Order of Luminis]]**: A secretive faction that is aware of the existence and location of the Sunken Citadel. This clandestine group has guarded the secrets of the citadel vor generations, operating in the shadows to protect the ancient knowledge and power contained within.

#### The Solar Core

Despite the catastrophic events, the **Solar Core** still functions, though its power has waned significantly over the centuries.

- **Residual Power:** The Solar Core continues to emit a faint protective aura, keeping the blight at bay in its immediate vicinity.
- **Maintenance Rituals:** Ancient rituals and spells, maintained by surviving guardians or automated enchantments, keep the core operational.
- **Energy Sources:** The core draws energy from deep within the earth, possibly tapping into ley lines or other mystical sources.

### Key Locations within the Citadel

1. **The Grand Hall:** The central chamber of the citadel, now partially collapsed and filled with rubble. Statues of ancient heroes and murals depicting the twin suns still stand, albeit worn and faded.
2. **The Solar Chamber:** The heart of the citadel, where the Solar Core resides. This chamber is heavily warded and protected, even now.
3. **The Archives:** A vast library containing ancient tomes and scrolls. Many are damaged, but some knowledge remains intact.
4. **The Guardian's Quarters:** Living spaces for the ethereal guardians and any surviving inhabitants. These areas are protected by powerful wards.
5. **The Golden Vault:** A hidden chamber containing legendary weaponry and artifacts created before the fall of Luminae. It is said to be protected by intricate traps and powerful magic.
