## Overview

In the middle of the woods near [[Willowbrook]] stands an old, crumbling tower, long abandoned and shrouded in mystery. The villagers speak of it in hushed tones, recalling the days when it was home to a mad lumberer named **[[Garrik the Woodcutter]]**, who vanished under strange circumstances. Now, the tower is a place of eerie silence and foreboding shadows.

## Setting the Scene
The players are sent to the Abandoned Tower by [[Elder Bramble]], who has heard #rumors of strange noises and lights emanating from the tower at night. She believes there might be useful supplies left behind by [[Garrik the Woodcutter|Garrik]] that could aid the village, and she is also concerned about potential dangers lurking there.
## The Approach
As the players make their way through the dense woods, they notice the signs of neglect and decay. The path is overgrown, and the trees seem to lean closer together, creating an almost tunnel-like effect. Eventually, they arrive at a small clearing where the tower stands.

**Description of the Tower:**
- The tower is about three stories high, made of weathered stone, with ivy and moss climbing its walls.
- The windows are shattered, and the door hangs loosely on its hinges.
- The air is thick with the scent of damp wood and earth, and a faint, almost imperceptible hum seems to emanate from the tower.
## Exploration and Clues
The players can explore the tower, finding various clues and items that tell the story of [[Garrik the Woodcutter|Garrik]] the Woodcutter.
### Ground Floor:   
- **Old Tools:** Rusted saws, #Axes , and other lumbering tools scattered around.
- **Journal Pages:** Torn and scattered pages from Garrik's journal, detailing his descent into madness as he claimed to hear the trees whispering to him.
- **Basic Supplies:** A few usable items such as rope, lanterns, and dried rations.

### Second Floor:
- **Living Quarters:** A dusty bedroll, broken furniture, and more journal pages.
- **Strange Carvings:** The walls are covered in strange symbols and carvings, possibly a mix of druidic runes and mad scribblings.
- **Garrik's Carving Knife:** A magical tool that can be used to carve protective symbols or used as a dagger that deals additional radiant damage against undead or blight entities.
- **Locked Chest:** A small chest with a simple lock (DC 10 Dexterity check with Thieves' Tools to open). Inside are some basic adventuring gear and a potion of healing.

### Third Floor:
- **Observation Room:** An open area with large windows offering a view of the surrounding woods. A rickety wooden ladder leads to the roof.
- **Final Journal Entry:** Garrik's last coherent entry, mentioning a "dark presence" in the woods and his fear that it was coming for him.

### The Confrontation
As the players reach the top of the tower, they hear a low growling sound. Suddenly, a spectral figure appears—a twisted, ghostly form of Garrik the Woodcutter, driven mad by the dark presence he feared.
- **Spectral Garrik:**
    - **Appearance:** A ghostly figure with wild, unkempt hair and eyes glowing with a sinister light. His ghostly form wields a spectral axe.
    - **Combat Encounter:** Spectral Garrik (use the statistics for a Specter with some modifications, such as an additional axe attack dealing necrotic damage).
    - **Tactics:** Garrik will attempt to frighten the players with his ghostly wails and will strike at those who try to open the locked chest or disturb his carvings.

### Additional Discoveries and Loot
- **Hidden Compartment:** In the floorboards of the observation room, players might find a hidden compartment (DC 12 Investigation check). Inside, they could discover:
    - **Wooden Figurines:** Hand-carved figurines representing the villagers of Willowbrook. Garrik might have been trying to protect them in his own way.
    - **Old Map:** A map of the surrounding forest with marked locations, possibly indicating places of interest or danger.
    - **Enchanted Amulet:** A simple amulet that offers minor protection (+1 to AC or advantage on saving throws against fear).
    - **Druidic Spell Scrolls:** Found among the journal pages, these scrolls contain spells that can aid in protecting against or combating the blight (e.g., Protection from Evil and Good, Lesser Restoration).

### Mysteries and Future Plot Hooks

- **The Dark Presence:** The final journal entry and the strange carvings hint at a dark presence in the woods. Players might need to investigate further to understand and possibly combat this threat.
- **Garrik's Legacy:** The wooden figurines and the hidden compartment suggest that Garrik had a deeper connection to the village. Players might uncover more about his life and his struggles, leading to future quests.
