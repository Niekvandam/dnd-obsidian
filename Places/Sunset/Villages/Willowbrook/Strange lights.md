**Overview:** The strange lights seen in the forest around Willowbrook are not just a natural phenomenon but the manifestation of **Will-o'-Wisps**—mysterious, ghostly lights that lure the unwary into danger. These particular wisps, however, are drawn to the ancient magic surrounding Willowbrook, especially the **Whispering Willow**.

**Plot Hook:** As the players settle into Willowbrook villagers talk about the lights that appear in the woods at night. The lights seem to move with purpose, leading some villagers to believe they are spirits or omens. One night, the players witness these lights firsthand. They appear to dance through the trees, beckoning anyone who gazes upon them.

An elderly villager, known for his superstitions, approaches the players with a request. His grandson went missing after following the lights into the woods, and he fears the worst. He begs the players to investigate the lights and, if possible, bring his grandson back safely.

**The Truth Behind the Lights:** The lights are indeed **Will-o'-Wisps**, but they have been drawn to Willowbrook by the residual magical energy of the **Whispering Willow**. These wisps are more than just malevolent spirits; they are connected to a forgotten part of Willowbrook's history—a time when the village was protected by ancient magic that has since faded.

As the players investigate the lights, they discover that the **Will-o'-Wisps** are attracted to something hidden deep in the forest. Perhaps it’s an old, magical relic tied to the **Whispering Willow**, or the location of a long-lost ritual site. The wisps guard this place fiercely, attacking any who come close.

**Possible Outcomes:**

- The players could uncover the relic or ritual site, gaining knowledge or a powerful artifact that ties into the larger story of the **Blight** and **Helionis**.
- They may learn more about the **Whispering Willow** and its connection to Willowbrook’s protection, setting the stage for future quests involving the **Herbalist's Circle**.
- The players could rescue the villager's grandson, gaining the trust and gratitude of the townsfolk, as well as a potential ally or guide for their future adventures.

**XP and Rewards:**

- **Investigating the Lights:** 50 XP per player.
- **Rescuing the Grandson:** 100 XP per player.
- **Uncovering the Relic/Ritual Site:** 150 XP per player, plus a magical item or piece of lore that ties into the campaign.
