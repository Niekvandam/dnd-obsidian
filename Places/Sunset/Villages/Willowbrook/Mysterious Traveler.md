# Description: 
The arrival of [[Liora Moonshadow]], an enigmatic elf ranger, brings tales of a spreading blight affecting nearby lands. She seeks aid from the village to combat this growing darkness, also called [[The Blight]]. [[Elder Bramble]] is suspicious of Liora, and is unsure what her intentions are here.

# Plot Hooks:
- **Blight Investigation:** The players can join Liora in investigating the source of the blight, leading to discoveries about an ancient curse or a corrupted nature spirit.
- **Expanding Threat:** As the players delve deeper, they find that the blight is part of a larger plot by an evil entity seeking to corrupt and control the entire region.
-  **Alliance Building:** The quest to combat the blight might require the players to seek help from other villages or factions, fostering alliances and uncovering hidden resources or allies.
