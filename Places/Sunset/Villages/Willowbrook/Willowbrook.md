# Willowbrook

![[Willowbrook.jpg]]
## Overview
Willowbrook is a small village located in a lush valley within the Willowbrook Region. Known for its fertile lands and the Annual Harvest Festival, it is home to around 150-200 residents.

## Appearance
- **General Aesthetic:** Quaint and picturesque with well-maintained cottages and blooming gardens.
- **Notable Landmarks:** The village square, the old willow tree, and the village well.

## Leadership and Governance
- **Leader:** [[Elder Bramble]] (Village Elder)
- **Government Type:** Village council led by [[Elder Bramble]]
- **Law Enforcement:** Village militia trained by [[Thorn Battlehammer]]

## Economy and Resources
- **Primary Industries:** Agriculture, herbalism
- **Notable Trades:** Farming, potion-making
- **Important Resources:** Fertile soil, abundant flora

## Population and Demographics
- **Population:** 150-200
- **Races and Cultures:** Predominantly humans and halflings, with a few dwarves and elves
- **Community Description:** Close-knit and friendly, with strong communal ties

## Key Locations
- **Village/Town Square:** The heart of the village, where the [[Annual Harvest Festival]] is held
- **Market:** A small marketplace with stalls selling local produce and crafts
- **Inn/Tavern:** The Resting Oak, run by [[Pippa Quickfoot]]
- **Smithy:** Operated by [[Thorn Battlehammer]]
- **Herbalist/Healer:** The herbalist's hut of [[Marigold Thistlefoot]]
- **Other Notable Locations:** The Elder's Hall, the abandoned tower
