# The Willowbrook Church

## Overview
The Willowbrook Church is a modest yet charming place of worship located at the heart of Willowbrook village. It serves as a spiritual center for the community, offering a sanctuary for reflection and communal gatherings.

## Flavor Description
The church is constructed from local stone and timber, with a steeple that reaches towards the sky. Vines and flowers adorn its exterior, giving it an inviting and serene appearance. Inside, wooden pews line the aisle leading up to an altar decorated with seasonal flowers and candles. Stained glass windows depict scenes of nature and harmony, casting colorful patterns on the floor when the sunlight streams through.

## Key People
### [[Father Elias]]
- **Role:** Head Priest
- **Description:** A kind and wise middle-aged man with a calming presence. Father Elias is known for his compassionate sermons and his dedication to helping the villagers in times of need.
- **Quirks:** Often seen tending to the church garden or reading ancient texts.

### [[Sister Mirabel]]
- **Role:** Assistant Priestess
- **Description:** A young and energetic woman with a deep love for music and art. Sister Mirabel leads the choir and is responsible for the beautiful hymns that fill the church during services.
- **Quirks:** Known for her talent in crafting intricate flower arrangements for the altar.

## Events

### Weekly Services
- **Description:** Held every seventh day, these gatherings include communal prayers, hymns led by Sister Mirabel, and sermons delivered by Father Elias. Villagers come together to seek guidance and solace.
### Seasonal Festivals
- **Description:** The church plays a key role in the village’s seasonal celebrations, such as the[[Annual Harvest Festival]] and the [[Spring Blossom Festival]]. These events often feature special ceremonies and blessings.
### Evening Vigil
- **Description:** On the first evening of each month, the church holds a candlelit vigil. Villagers gather to offer prayers and light candles in remembrance of loved ones and in hopes of blessings for the month ahead.

## Mysteries and Loot
### Entering the Hidden Reliquary
To enter the Hidden Reliquary beneath the Willowbrook Church, the players must first uncover and solve a series of clues and challenges that test their purity of heart and strength of will.

#### Step 1: The Whispering Walls
- **Interaction:** At night, when the church is quiet and still, the players can hear faint whispers emanating from the church walls. These whispers are messages from the spirits of the past, offering guidance or warnings.
- **Skill Check:** Players must make a successful Wisdom (Perception) check (DC 15) to understand the whispers. If successful, they hear a cryptic message: “Seek the light that does not burn, beneath the roots of the sacred tree.”

#### Step 2: The Sacred Grove
- **Interaction:** Behind the church is a small, sacred grove where Father Elias often meditates. The players must visit this grove and perform a small ritual to commune with the spirits.
- **Ritual:** The players need to light incense and offer a prayer for guidance. This requires a successful Wisdom (Religion) check (DC 13).
- **Clue:** Upon completing the ritual, a faint glow appears around the roots of the largest tree, revealing an inscription: “In the heart of faith, under the altar, the path is revealed.”

#### Step 3: The Altar
- **Interaction:** The players return to the church and investigate the altar. They must search for a hidden mechanism or clue that reveals the entrance to the reliquary.
- **Skill Check:** Players must make an Intelligence (Investigation) check (DC 14) to find a hidden switch or lever beneath the altar.
- **Result:** Activating the mechanism causes a section of the floor to shift, revealing a hidden staircase leading down into the depths of the church.

#### Step 4: The Test of Purity and Will
- **Interaction:** As the players descend into the reliquary, they encounter a magical barrier that tests their purity of heart and strength of will.
- **Challenge:** Each player must confront a vision of their greatest fear or guilt. This requires a successful Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) to overcome.
- **Outcome:** If they succeed, the barrier dissipates, allowing them to enter the hidden chamber. If they fail, they are temporarily paralyzed with fear, but can attempt the saving throw again after a short rest.

#### Inside the Hidden Reliquary

- **Description:** The hidden chamber is dimly lit by an ethereal glow. Ancient relics and treasures are carefully arranged on stone pedestals and shelves. The air is cool and carries the faint scent of incense.
- **Loot:**
    - **Holy Symbol of Protection:** Grants the bearer a +1 bonus to AC.
    - **Healing Potions:** A set of three potions that heal 2d4+2 HP each.
    - **Ancient Scrolls:** Contain forgotten prayers and minor spells that could be of use to the players.
- **Mystery:** An ancient inscription on the wall reads: “Guardians of the light, protectors of the faith, our secrets keep us strong. When the dark stones of blight arise, let our prayers and purity be the shield. May the pure of heart carry our legacy and cleanse the land.”

## Conclusion
The Willowbrook Church is more than just a place of worship; it is a cornerstone of the community, rich with history and secrets waiting to be discovered. Its serene atmosphere and the guidance of its kind-hearted clergy make it a sanctuary for all who seek peace and understanding.
