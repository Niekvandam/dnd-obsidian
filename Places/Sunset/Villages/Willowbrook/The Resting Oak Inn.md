The **Resting Oak Inn**, run by [[Pippa Quickfoot]], is a central hub of activity and a place where adventurers can rest, gather information, and interact with the locals of [[Willowbrook]]. Here are some activities that the players can undertake during their stay:

#### 1. **Socializing with Locals**

- **Meeting Villagers:** The players can mingle with the locals, learning more about the village and its history. They can hear firsthand accounts of the current issues like the missing livestock and strange lights in the forest.
- **Rumors and Stories:** By chatting with patrons, players can gather rumors and stories that may lead to new quests or provide valuable information.

#### 2. **Games and Entertainment**

- **Dice and Card Games:** The inn offers a variety of simple games that the villagers enjoy. Players can participate in games like "Meet the Ends" (previously described) to win or lose coins.
- **Tales and Songs:** Some nights, local bards or traveling minstrels perform at the inn. Players can listen to tales of heroism, ancient legends, and enjoy music.  

#### 3. **Quests and Plot Hooks**

- **Message Board:** The inn has a message board where villagers post requests for help. Tasks can range from finding lost items to more dangerous missions like investigating the strange lights in the forest.
- **Mysterious Travelers:** [[Liora Moonshadow]] might approach the players with a plea for assistance, leading to a more significant quest related to the blight.

#### 4. **Information Gathering**
- **Talking to Pippa Quickfoot:** As the innkeeper, Pippa hears all sorts of news and gossip. She can provide valuable insights and may have some tasks that need doing around the village.
- **Interacting with [[Dorian Moonrise]]:** As a night-caster, Dorian might be found at the inn, offering wisdom and potentially teaching the players about illusion magic.

#### 5. **Training and Skill Improvement**
- **Learning New Skills:** Some villagers might offer to teach the players new skills or improve their existing ones. For example, a local hunter could teach tracking skills, or [[Thorn Battlehammer]] could give combat training.
- **Herbalism Lessons:** [[Marigold Thistlefoot]] might offer lessons in herbalism and potion-making, giving the players a chance to learn about local flora and its uses.

#### 6. **Rest and Recuperation**
- **Comfortable Lodging:** The inn provides a safe and comfortable place to rest, allowing the players to recover from their travels and prepare for upcoming adventures.
- **Hearty Meals:** Players can enjoy hearty meals that restore their health and boost their spirits. Special dishes made with local ingredients might offer temporary buffs.

#### 7. **Crafting and Commerce**
- **Trading Goods:** Players can trade or sell items they have found on their travels. Local craftsmen might be interested in purchasing rare materials.
- **Crafting and Repairs:** The inn has a small workshop area where players can craft simple items or repair their gear. They can also commission local craftsmen for more complex items.

#### 8. **Mysterious Happenings**
- **Ghost Stories:** On certain nights, eerie tales about the Whispering Woods and the old willow tree are shared. These stories might hint at deeper mysteries and hidden dangers.
- **Unexpected Visitors:** Occasionally, the inn might receive unusual visitors who bring with them strange and exciting opportunities or challenges.
