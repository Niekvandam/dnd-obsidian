### Current Issues and Plot Hooks

#### Missing Livestock

**Description:** Farmers in Willowbrook have been reporting the mysterious disappearance of their livestock. Cows, sheep, and even chickens are vanishing without a trace, causing panic and fear among the villagers. [[Elder Bramble]] has seen a group of goblins roaming around at night. Could these be the cuprit?

## Plot Hooks

### Hidden Threat:
The livestock thefts could be linked to a goblin tribe hiding in the nearby [[Hidden cave system]], leading players to explore the caves and uncover the threat.
