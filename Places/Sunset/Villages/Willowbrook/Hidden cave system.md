A cave system, in the Northwest of Willowbrook, which can be found near the edge of the [[Everspring River]]. The Hidden cave system contains a faction of Goblins, which have been stealing nearby livestock to keep themselves alive. These goblins are harvesting [[Blight Gems]] from the cave in order to power the blight entity that is roaming around the Willowbrook region. 


## Properties
**Brittle**: 
- The cave is old and the rocks are brittle. If the party makes too much noise, there is a possibility that rocks from above will fall down below. 
**Luscious**: 
- The Everspring river flowing through this cave allows floral live to thrive, as well as some curious cave critters that can be found.
**Trapped**: 
- Goblins have boobytrapped the area. Watch out!
**Visions of blight**:
- Influences of the blight can be found in the cave, causing delirious hallucinations at random. These are mild, but can get worse if exposed to the [[Blight Gems]] for longer. 
# Progression

## Cave Entrance

**Description:**
- The entrance to the cave is concealed by dense foliage and rocks. It's a narrow passage that opens up into a larger cavern. 
- Moss-covered stones, faint sounds of flowing water, dim light filtering through cracks. 

Whilst the party enters the cave, the terrain is tough to navigate, and does not provide any suitable terrain for running or acrobatics.
\
## Left section
If the party opts to go to the left, they will end up in a long hallway, filled with two traps. A spike trap and a poison dart trap. If the party survives these two traps, they will end up at a massive rock door. They cannot continue this route unless they force open the door. 

## First cavern
A spacious cavern with stalactites and stalagmites. The air is damp, and the floor is slippery with moisture. Roll 1d10, if > 5, dexterity check to proper attack. Otherwise, half effectiveness of action.

This cavern contains two goblin scouts on lookout, which are distracted, throwing rocks in water. If the party makes too much noise, it is possible that two more goblin reinforcements come on in during the battle.

## Everspring River Section
The cavern widens up, and a grassy area exposes itself. A river rushing down the middle with patches of grass on both sides. In front of them, a giant mushroom can be seen. The water in the middle is clear and cold.

There are two goblin patrols (both of 2 goblins) walking back and forth.


## Underground lake
If the Everspring River is followed in the appropriate direction, they will end up at a bridge, followed by an underground lake. The river feeds into and out of this lake, where it splits in two. The lake is lit up by bioluminescent flowers, hanging from the walls.  

If the party pays attention, they can spot a mysterious fishing line hanging from the wall of the lake, in some kind of balcony. This belongs to [[Karpo]], a Rogue Fisherman who loves fishing but is terribly afraid of goblins. 

Near the edge of the lake, a group of goblin fisherman can be found passionately fishing, and discussing the size of their recently caught fish. 

## Hidden guardian cave
Deep in the cave is a Heartstone. It was once a pure crystal, much like the [[Moonlight Crystals]], but has been corrupted by the blight. This corruption gives it unique properties that, when cleansed and harnessed can amplify [[Dorian Moonrise]]'s illusion magic and stabilize the night dome over Willowbrook.

In the cave roams a [[Blight Entity]], called the [[Blighted Cave Guardian]]. 
## Goblin Settlement

If the party continues past the lake, they will encounter a group of statues to their left. If the statues are followed, they will end up at the goblin settlement, where the chieftain resides. the area is heavily guarded and more organized. 

There are groups of tents, with even more soldiers. Additionally, the party can encounter the stolen livestock penned up in a collection of cages. \

### Encounters: 
- 6 goblins
- Goblin Boss

## Goblin mine
Near the end of the River, a group of goblins that are mining can be found. These goblins are walking up and down, harvesting deep blue crystals known as [[Blight Gems]]. These gems are infused with dark energy and have the ability to communicate with otherwordly deities or entities linked to the blight. These goblins are mining these gems to harness their power. 
## Goblin Lair

The heart of the goblin settlement is where the chieftain resides. The area is heavily guarded and more organized. There are larger huts, a throne-like seat for the chieftain, and stolen goods piled up. 

### Encounters:
- Goblin Chieftain
- Elite guards
- Goblin shaman 
- Goblins
