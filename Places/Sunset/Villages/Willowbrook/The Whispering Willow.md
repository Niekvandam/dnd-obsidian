A great ancient tree, 2000 years old, in the Centre of [[Willowbrook]], that the [[The herbalist's Circle]] uses for its prayers and incanations.

It is said that the great willow whispers secrets of the ancient world to the circle members. 

Additionally, there is the legend of [[The Guardian's Blessing]]. A blighted plague erupted in Willowbrook during the [[The two suns#The fall of Lunaris (700 years ago)]] . The [[Blight Entity]] that appeared thanks to the [[Blight Gems]] was banished thanks to the [[The Guardian's Blessing]]. 

Inside the Whispering willow is said to be a guardian, which was grown under the rays of pure [[Helionis]]. (Unbeknownst to most of the population of Willowbrook)
