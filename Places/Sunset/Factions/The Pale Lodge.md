## The Pale Lodge: Interdimensional Godhunters

**Faction Name:** The Pale Lodge

**Core Belief:** No deity, however powerful, is beyond justice. They believe divine power unchecked breeds corruption and tyranny across realities. They are not necessarily _evil_, but they are ruthlessly pragmatic and see the potential for good and evil in all beings, even gods. Their primary goal is to maintain balance by removing deities who overstep their bounds and threaten the delicate cosmic order.

**Goals:**
- **Hunt and Eliminate Rogue Deities:** Their primary focus is targeting gods and demi-gods who abuse their power, interfere excessively in mortal affairs, or become corrupt and tyrannical. This can range from benevolent dictators gone wrong to outright malevolent entities.
- **Maintain the Balance:** The Pale Lodge believes in a delicate balance of power between the planes. They strive to prevent any one deity or force from becoming too dominant. They are less concerned with worship or belief and more concerned with actual power and influence.
- **Gather Knowledge:** They constantly seek knowledge about deities, their weaknesses, their domains, and the intricate workings of the multiverse. This knowledge is crucial for planning their hunts and understanding the consequences of their actions.
- **Preserve the Veil:** While they hunt gods, they understand the necessity of some divine influence. They are careful not to disrupt the fundamental fabric of reality, and actively work to prevent planar rifts and incursions caused by unchecked divine power.

**Structure:**
- **The Grand Huntmaster:** The leader of the Pale Lodge, chosen for their skill, wisdom, and ruthlessness. Their identity is often shrouded in mystery, and they rarely appear in public.
- **The Conclave:** A council of high-ranking Hunters who strategize, plan hunts, and oversee the Lodge's operations. They are experts in various fields, from arcane lore to combat tactics.
- **Hunters:** The rank and file of the Pale Lodge. They are skilled warriors, mages, rogues, and scholars, each specializing in different aspects of god hunting. They are often recruited from various planes and backgrounds, united by their dedication to the cause.
- **Seekers:** Scholars and researchers who gather information about deities, their followers, and their domains. They provide crucial intelligence for planning hunts.
- **Wardens:** Members responsible for maintaining the Lodge's hidden strongholds across the multiverse and safeguarding their vast library of knowledge.

**Appearance and Symbolism:**
- **Pale Attire:** Members of the Pale Lodge often wear pale or grey clothing, symbolizing their neutrality and the pallor of death they bring to their targets.
- **The Obsidian Shard:** The symbol of the Pale Lodge is an obsidian shard, representing the fragility of divine power and the Lodge's ability to shatter it. It is often worn as a pendant or incorporated into their weapons.
- **Hidden Strongholds:** The Pale Lodge operates from hidden strongholds scattered across the multiverse, shielded by powerful magic and concealed from divine eyes. These strongholds serve as bases of operation, libraries, and training grounds.

**Interaction with the World:**
- **Secrecy:** The Pale Lodge operates in utmost secrecy, as their activities are highly dangerous and could draw the wrath of powerful deities. They often use aliases and operate through intermediaries.
- **Neutrality:** They maintain a neutral stance in most conflicts, unless a deity's actions threaten the balance of power. They are willing to work with mortals, celestials, or even devils if it serves their goals.
- **Resources:** The Pale Lodge possesses vast resources, acquired through centuries of hunting and careful management. They have access to powerful artifacts, arcane knowledge, and a network of informants across countless planes.

**Possible Hooks for Players:**
- **Recruitment:** Players might be recruited into the Pale Lodge if they possess skills or knowledge that are valuable to the cause.
- **Investigation:** Players might stumble upon the Pale Lodge while investigating a powerful deity or a planar disturbance.
- **Conflict:** Players might find themselves opposed to the Pale Lodge if their goals clash with the Lodge's agenda. Perhaps they are protecting a deity the Lodge has targeted, or they disagree with the Lodge's methods.
- **Alliance:** Players might forge an alliance with the Pale Lodge to combat a common threat, such as a rogue god or a planar invasion.
