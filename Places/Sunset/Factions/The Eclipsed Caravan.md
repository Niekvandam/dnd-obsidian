
**Overview:** The **Eclipsed Caravan** is a ruthless and highly skilled faction of bounty hunters operating across the world of Sunset. They are known for their efficiency, secrecy, and willingness to take on any job, no matter how dangerous or morally ambiguous, as long as the price is right. The members of the Crimson Caravan are diverse, consisting of skilled trackers, fighters, and magic users who specialize in hunting down animals, people, and rare items. They travel in well-armed caravans, setting up temporary camps near villages and towns where they can find work.

**Structure and Organization:**
- **Guildmasters:** The leaders of the Crimson Caravan, usually former bounty hunters who have earned their place through skill, loyalty, and a proven track record. They coordinate missions, distribute payments, and maintain order within the faction.

- **Hunters:** The backbone of the faction, these are the skilled individuals who carry out bounties. They are divided into specialized groups based on their expertise: Beast Hunters, Manhunters, and Relic Seekers.

- **Beast Hunters:** Experts in tracking and capturing or killing dangerous animals and magical creatures. They are often hired to protect villages from beasts affected by the blight or to capture rare creatures for wealthy collectors.

- **Manhunters:** Specialists in tracking down wanted criminals, escapees, or specific individuals. They operate with a cold efficiency, using a variety of skills to apprehend their targets, whether dead or alive.

- **Relic Seekers:** Treasure hunters and archaeologists turned mercenaries, these individuals specialize in finding and retrieving rare items, artifacts, or relics. They often venture into dangerous and blighted territories in search of valuable objects.

- **Caravan Guards:** Every caravan has its own group of guards, tasked with protecting the faction's camps, wagons, and supplies. These guards are usually seasoned fighters and are well-prepared for ambushes and attacks from both creatures and other factions.

**Operations:**
- **Bounty Boards:** The Crimson Caravan maintains bounty boards in several villages and towns, listing available jobs for local hunters and residents. These boards are updated regularly with new bounties for beasts, individuals, or rare items.

- **Mobile Camps:** The faction's caravans are well-fortified and designed for mobility, allowing them to set up temporary camps quickly. These camps serve as bases of operation for local bounty hunting activities and are usually located just outside village borders.

- **Contracts:** The Crimson Caravan takes contracts from a variety of clients, including village leaders, wealthy merchants, other factions, and occasionally, secretive or shady individuals. They operate under strict confidentiality, **never revealing their clients unless directly challenged.**

**Goals:**
- **Profit:** Above all, the Crimson Caravan is driven by profit. They take the highest paying jobs and are known for their willingness to negotiate, but they rarely work for free or out of goodwilll
- **Reputation:** The faction values its reputation as the most efficient and capable bounty hunters in Sunset. They aim to maintain their standing by taking on difficult jobs and succeeding where others fail.
- **Influence:** By controlling bounty boards and providing services to villages and towns, the Crimson Caravan maintains a level of influence over local politics and events. They use this influence to secure more contracts and ensure a steady flow of work.

**Notable Members:**
- **Ragnar Thorne:** A legendary Manhunter and one of the founding members of the Crimson Caravan. He is known for his ruthless efficiency and his ability to track targets across vast distances. Ragnar has a strict code of honor and believes in completing every contract, no matter the cost.
- **Silva Ravenspire:** A former druid turned Beast Hunter, Silva has an uncanny ability to communicate with animals and track them through the wilderness. She has a deep hatred for the blight and takes on jobs specifically targeting blighted creatures whenever possible.
- **Finnian "Fingers" O'Malley:** A skilled thief and Relic Seeker, Finnian specializes in finding and retrieving valuable items from heavily guarded or dangerous locations. He is known for his charm, wit, and ability to get out of tight situations.
