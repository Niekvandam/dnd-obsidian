**Overview:**  
The Order of the Twin Suns is a religious organization dedicated to the worship of the two celestial bodies, **Helionis** and **Lunaris**. Originally a peaceful and harmonious order, the corruption of Helionis by **Umbraxis** has caused a schism within the order. Now, factions within the order are divided between those who still revere Helionis and those who believe that the only way to save the world is to restore the balance by finding Lunaris or a new celestial power to replace the corrupted sun.

**Goals:**
- To uncover the truth behind the fall of Lunaris and the corruption of Helionis.
- To seek out ancient prophecies and relics that might aid in restoring balance to the cosmos.
- To protect the faithful and convert others to their cause.

**Methods:**
- Pilgrimages and expeditions to ancient ruins and celestial observatories.
- Conducting sacred rituals and prayers to gain favor or insight from the celestial beings.
- Infiltration and influence within other factions to gain resources and information.
