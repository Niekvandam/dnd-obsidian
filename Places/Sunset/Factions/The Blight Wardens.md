**Overview:**  
The Blight Wardens are a secretive group dedicated to combating the spread of the blight and uncovering its origins. Composed of skilled druids, rangers, and nature clerics, the Wardens operate from hidden sanctuaries scattered across Sunset. They believe that the blight is a corruption of nature that must be purged to restore balance to the world.

**Goals:**
- To find and destroy the source of the blight.
- To purify corrupted lands and protect untainted regions.
- To recover lost knowledge and ancient rituals that can help in combating the blight.

**Methods:**
- Use of nature magic and druidic rituals to cleanse corrupted areas.
- Covert operations to destroy blighted creatures and Blight Gems.
- Gathering intelligence and forming alliances with other factions and local communities.
