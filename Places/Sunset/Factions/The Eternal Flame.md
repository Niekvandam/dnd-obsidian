**Overview:**  
The Eternal Flame is a militant order that believes in the purging fire as the ultimate solution to the blight and all other forms of corruption. They see themselves as the chosen warriors of Helionis, tasked with cleansing the world with holy fire. The order is based in fortified monasteries and conducts frequent raids against perceived threats.

**Goals:**

- To eradicate the blight and all other forms of corruption with purifying fire.
- To convert others to their faith and expand their influence across Sunset.
- To destroy any faction or force that opposes their vision of a purified world.

**Methods:**

- Conducting holy crusades against blighted areas and their inhabitants.
- Using fire magic and pyrotechnics to cleanse corrupted lands and artifacts.
- Engaging in aggressive proselytizing and recruitment campaigns.
