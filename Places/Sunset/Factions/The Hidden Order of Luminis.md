**Overview:**  
The Hidden Order of Luminis is a secretive faction that claims to be the last guardians of the knowledge and artifacts of the **Sunken Citadel**. They believe that the Solar Core and the relics created from it hold the key to combating the blight and restoring balance to Sunset. The order is composed of descendants of the original inhabitants of the Sunken Citadel and others who have been initiated into their ranks.

**Goals:**

- To protect and preserve the secrets of the Sunken Citadel.
- To recover and safeguard artifacts and knowledge related to the Solar Core.
- To use their knowledge and resources to oppose the blight and other threats to Sunset.

**Methods:**

- Operating in secrecy, using hidden bases and coded communications.
- Recruiting from among the descendants of the Sunken Citadel and other trusted individuals.
- Conducting covert missions to recover lost artifacts and eliminate threats to their cause.
