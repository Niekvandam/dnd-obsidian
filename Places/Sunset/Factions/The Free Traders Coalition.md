**Overview:**  
The Free Traders Coalition is a loose alliance of merchants, traders, and mercenaries who thrive on commerce and the exchange of goods. They operate across the known world of Sunset, often dealing in rare and valuable items, including Heartstones and Blight Gems. While not a force for good or evil, they are primarily motivated by profit and will work with or against other factions depending on where their interests align.

**Goals:**

- To maintain and expand trade routes across Sunset.
- To acquire and sell rare goods and artifacts, regardless of their origin.
- To protect their interests from bandits, pirates, and other factions.

**Methods:**

- Forming trade alliances and negotiating favorable deals.
- Hiring mercenaries and private guards to protect their caravans and ships.
- Engaging in smuggling, espionage, or sabotage to gain an advantage over competitors.
