### Overview
**Night-casters** are powerful illusionists in the world of [[Sunset]] who conjure local domes of night to provide the inhabitants with a semblance of the natural day and night cycle. In a realm covered by the eternal twilight of [[Helionis]], night-casters play a crucial role in maintaining the well-being and morale of their communities.

### Becoming a Night-Caster

#### Training and Apprenticeship

- **Selection:** Potential night-casters are identified at a young age based on their affinity for illusion magic and their innate magical abilities.
- **Apprenticeship:** Selected individuals undergo rigorous training under the guidance of a master night-caster. This apprenticeship can last many years and includes:
    - **Study of Illusion Magic:** Learning advanced illusion spells and techniques.
    - **Practical Application:** Practicing the creation and maintenance of the night dome, often starting with smaller, simpler illusions and gradually progressing to more complex ones.

#### Schools of Magic

- **Primary School:** #Illusion
    - Night-casters specialize in illusion magic, creating realistic and immersive illusions of night, complete with stars and a phantom moon.
- **Secondary Schools:** Enchantment and Abjuration
    - **Enchantment:** Used to enhance the calming and restful effects of the night dome, ensuring the psychological well-being of the villagers.
    - **Abjuration:** Employed to protect the night dome from external magical interference and to maintain its stability.

#### Basic spells
- **Minor Illusion:**
- **Description:** Creates a sound or an image of an object within range. The illusion lasts for up to 1 minute and can be used to distract or deceive.

- **Prestidigitation:**
- **Description:** A versatile cantrip that allows the caster to create small, magical effects such as lighting a candle, cleaning an object, or producing a harmless sensory effect.

- **Dancing Lights:**
- **Description:** Summons up to four torch-sized lights that float and move as the caster wills. These lights can appear as torches, lanterns, or glowing orbs.

- **Mage Hand:**
- **Description:** Conjures a spectral, floating hand that can manipulate objects, open doors, and carry items up to 10 pounds, all within the caster’s range.
#### Common Spells

- **1st Level:** Disguise Self, Silent Image, Sleep, Shield
- **2nd Level:** Mirror Image, Invisibility, Hold Person, Magic Mouth
- **3rd Level:** Major Image, Hypnotic Pattern, Counterspell, Dispel Magic
- **4th Level:** Greater Invisibility, Phantasmal Killer, Banishment, Resilient Sphere
- **5th Level:** Mislead, Seeming, Dominate Person, Wall of Force
#### Special Abilities
- **Create Night Dome (3rd level):** As an action, the night-caster can create a dome of night that covers a 100-foot radius, lasting for 8 hours. Within the dome, the area is treated as nighttime, complete with a starry sky and a phantom moon. This requires concentration.
- **Soothe Mind (5th level):** The night-caster can cast the Calm Emotions spell without expending a spell slot once per long rest.
- **Illusory Defense (7th level):** The night-caster gains the ability to cast Mirror Image as a reaction when targeted by an attack, once per long rest.
- **Aura of Tranquility (9th level):** Allies within the night dome gain advantage on Wisdom saving throws and recover from exhaustion at twice the normal rate.
### Rules and Responsibilities of Night-Casters
#### Responsibilities

- **Maintaining the Night Dome:** The primary duty of a night-caster is to create and sustain a magical dome of darkness over their village each night.
- **Community Well-Being:** Night-casters are responsible for ensuring that the night dome promotes rest and rejuvenation for the villagers. This includes adjusting the intensity and nature of the illusion to suit the community's needs.
- **Cultural Guardians:** They uphold the traditions and rituals associated with the artificial night, often leading or participating in ceremonies and festivals.

#### Rules and Conduct

- **Commitment to Service:** Night-casters must prioritize the well-being of their community above personal gain or ambition.
- **Ethical Use of Magic:** The use of illusion magic must adhere to strict ethical guidelines, avoiding manipulation or deceit outside of the night dome's creation.
- **Continuous Learning:** Night-casters are expected to continually refine their skills and knowledge, often seeking out ancient texts or other night-casters to learn from.
- **Secrecy of Techniques:** The specific techniques and spells used to create the night dome are closely guarded secrets, passed down only to apprentices within the order.

### Notable Night-Casters

- **[[Elder Illuma]]:** A renowned night-caster known for her particularly soothing and realistic night domes, enhancing the well-being of her village significantly.
- **[[Master Noctis]]:** A strict but wise master who has trained many successful night-casters and is deeply respected within the community.
- **[[Apprentice Liona]]:** A promising young illusionist currently in training, showing exceptional talent and potential for innovation in night-casting techniques.
