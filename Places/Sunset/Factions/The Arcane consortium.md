**Overview:**  
The Arcane Consortium is a powerful guild of wizards, sorcerers, and scholars dedicated to the pursuit of magical knowledge and power. They believe that understanding the arcane arts is key to controlling and possibly eradicating the blight. The Consortium operates out of major cities and hidden enclaves, hoarding magical artifacts and conducting dangerous experiments.

**Goals:**

- To collect and study magical artifacts, including Blight Gems and Heartstones.
- To master and control powerful spells and rituals that can manipulate the blight.
- To establish dominance over other magical organizations and become the foremost authority on arcane matters.

**Methods:**

- Funding expeditions to recover lost knowledge and powerful artifacts.
- Experimenting with dangerous magic, sometimes with little regard for consequences.
- Politically manipulating kingdoms and city-states to gain power and resources.
