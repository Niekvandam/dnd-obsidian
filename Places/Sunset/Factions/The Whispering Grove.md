**Overview:**  
The Whispering Grove is a druidic circle that believes the blight is a punishment from nature itself for the sins of civilization. They are dedicated to returning the world to a more natural state, free from the corruption of magic and the greed of mankind. The Grove operates in remote areas, where they work to protect and restore the natural world.

**Goals:**

- To cleanse the world of the blight and restore balance to nature.
- To oppose the use of magic and technology that they see as corrupting influences.
- To protect sacred sites and natural wonders from exploitation.

**Methods:**

- Conducting rituals and ceremonies to appease nature spirits and cleanse corrupted lands.
- Sabotaging industrial operations and magical experiments that harm the environment.
- Recruiting followers from among those who have been disillusioned by civilization’s excesses.
