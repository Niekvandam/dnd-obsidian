The world is that the adventurers play in is called Sunset. The world has been covered in eternal twilight for centuries, since the coming of [[Helionis]]. 

In the world of Sunset lies the [[The Sunken Citadel]], an old capital of the world of Sunset, tasked with keeping the rays of Helionis pure. 

## Properties
### Eternal twilight
As long as Helionis resides in Sunset, the sun will not set. For as the sun sets, the world will be able to see the full scale of Helionis. 
Residents of Sunset, however, still desire the natural cycle of night and day. To fulfill this need, each village has its own **night-casters**, powerful illusionists who conjure local domes of night.
### Night-Casters

**[[Night-casters]]** are esteemed members of each community, gifted in the art of powerful illusion magic. Their role is crucial for maintaining the semblance of night in a world dominated by eternal twilight. Each night-caster is responsible for creating and sustaining a magical dome of darkness over their village, allowing the inhabitants to experience night.

- **Illusion Magic:** Night-casters use complex spells to create a realistic illusion of night, complete with a starry sky and the gentle glow of a phantom moon.
- **Community Role:** They are highly respected and often hold a position of authority within their villages. The quality of their illusions can affect the well-being and morale of the villagers.
- **Training:** Becoming a night-caster requires rigorous training in illusion magic and a deep understanding of the celestial lore of Sunset.
#### Timekeeping

Even without a natural moon cycle, the residents of Sunset have developed their own methods of keeping time and day.

- **Clocks and Sundials:** Ingenious devices and sundials adapted to work under the constant twilight help the residents maintain their daily routines.
- **Cultural Practices:** Rituals and traditions help mark the passage of time, with festivals and ceremonies held to celebrate the phases of their artificial day and night.

### Legends
**Story of the two suns**: Some elder elves proclaim that back in their days, the world had two suns, and that there was a GREAT capital somewhere where the power of these two suns was harvested. However, not many people believe this myth.
